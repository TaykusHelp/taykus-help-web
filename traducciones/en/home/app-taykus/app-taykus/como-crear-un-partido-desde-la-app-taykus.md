# How to create a match from the Taykus app

As well as making bookings, the Taykus app lets players create matches so that other users can join and complete the group.

This option is useful when a player wants to organise a match but doesn't have all the players confirmed yet. From the app they can select the club, the date, the time and the court features, and set the details needed to publish the match.

### Explainer video

{% embed url="https://youtube.com/shorts/f-GP1juEfDE?feature=share" %}

Follow these steps:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Date and time

* Choose the day in the **Date** bar.
*   Select the time you want to play.

    > 📷 **SCREENSHOT 3 pending:** app, row of days and times.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Gender, match type and level

> 📷 **SCREENSHOT 4 pending:** app, Gender, Match type and level, and Price and duration.

* **Gender:** you see **Open**, **Mixed** and your own gender (**Male** or **Female**).
* **Match type:** **Competitive** (the result changes your level) or **Friendly** (it doesn't).
* **Level:** the slider starts with a range around your level. Move the two points to choose the minimum and maximum level of the players who can join.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Price and duration

* Choose the court features (for example, Outdoor, Panoramic, Double) and the duration (for example, 60 or 90 minutes).
* The price shown is what each player pays.

> You don't choose a specific court: the match is placed on the virtual court **Matches** and, once it's full, it moves to a free real court. To find out more, see [How matches work in the calendar](https://app.gitbook.com/s/hSQoRJmdDmzxYVh7vpFW/calendario/calendario/partidos/como-funcionan-los-partidos-en-el-calendario).
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Create the match

* Click **Create match**.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

To find out more about each option, see [Match types: competitive, friendly and gender](https://app.gitbook.com/s/hSQoRJmdDmzxYVh7vpFW/calendario/calendario/partidos/tipos-de-partido).
