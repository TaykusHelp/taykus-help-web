# How to invite Taykus to your Google Play Console account

So that we can help you set up or manage your Android app, you need to invite the Taykus team to your **Google Play Console** account.

On this platform, Google lets you manage users, permissions and access for apps that are published or in the process of being published.

***

### Download the invitation guide

You can download the guide with screenshots from the following link:

{% file src="../../.gitbook/assets/Guía de invitación a Play Store.pdf" %}

> **Important:** for security reasons, the file may be password-protected.\
> If you don't have the password, ask the Taykus team for it.

***

Once you've sent the invitation, the Taykus team can accept the access and carry on with the necessary management or setup.
