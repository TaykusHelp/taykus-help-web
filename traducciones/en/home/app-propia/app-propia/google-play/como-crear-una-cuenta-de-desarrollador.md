# How to create a developer account

To publish your own app on Google Play, you need a developer account in **Google Play Console**.

This account must be linked to the company, club or entity responsible for the app, as Google will ask for information to verify the organisation and complete the registration correctly.

In this article we'll look at how to create a developer account for Android and what information you need to have ready before completing the registration.

***

### Before you start

Before you begin the registration, we recommend having the following information ready:

* A Gmail account linked to the company, the club or one of its managers.
* The company's or entity's **D-U-N-S** number.
* Contact phone number and email address.
* The club's or entity's official website.
* Billing details.
* A payment method to pay the registration fee.
* The developer name that will be shown publicly on Google Play.

It's important to use details that are up to date and checked regularly, as Google may use them to verify the account or send important communications.

***

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to the registration page

* Go to the following link:\
  <a href="https://play.google.com/console/u/0/signup." class="button primary">Go to Google Play Console</a>
*   Once there, sign in with a Gmail account that belongs to the company or to one of the club's directors.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Select the organisation account type

* On the registration start screen, select **An organisation or business** as the account type.
*   Click **Get started** to begin setting up the account.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Information needed to create the account

* Google shows a summary of the information you'll need to continue with the registration.
* The details it may ask for include:
  * The company's **D-U-N-S** number.
  * Contact phone number or email address.
  *   A payment method to pay the registration fee.<br>

      <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>



#### D-U-N-S number

* The **D-U-N-S** number is a unique company identifier that Google may ask for to verify the organisation.
* If the company or entity doesn't have this number yet, you'll need to request it before continuing with the account setup by clicking the following button:\
  <a href="https://cloud.email.informa.es/numero-duns" class="button primary">Request a D-U-N-S number</a>
* There are two options:
  * **Priority request:** you get the number immediately for a fee of €9 + VAT.
  * **Free request:** there is no cost, but the number takes 10 to 14 days to be issued.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (3).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Enter the developer name

* Next, enter the **developer name** you want to link to the account.
*   This name will be shown publicly on Google Play as the publisher of your apps, so it should properly represent the company, club or entity.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (4).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Create the payments profile

*   During registration, Google will ask you to create or link a **payments profile**.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (5).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* This profile is needed to verify the organisation's identity and manage the billing information linked to the account.
* Fill in the details requested, such as the company's or entity's tax or billing information.\
  ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (6).png>)
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Organisation information

*   In this section, Google will ask for additional information about the organisation.

    Some of the details it may ask for are:

    * Size of the organisation.
    * Contact phone number.
    * Official website.
*   It's important that the details you enter match the official information of the company or entity, to avoid errors during verification.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (7).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Developer information

*   Google may also ask for information about the person or entity that will manage the developer account.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (8).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* In this section you may be asked about:
  * Previous experience developing or publishing apps.
  * The company's or entity's website.
  * Professional profile or additional information about the developer.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (9).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Information about the apps

*   In this step you need to give information about the apps that will be published on Google Play.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (10).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   Google may ask for details such as:

    * The type of apps you will develop.
    * The main purpose of the app.
    * The app category.
    * Whether you expect to earn money from the app.
    * Whether there will be in-app purchases, advertising or other ways of making money.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (11).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* For a club's own app, you can say that it is a services or sports management app aimed at the club's users.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Contact information for Google

*   Before finishing, you need to fill in the contact details Google will use to communicate with the company or entity.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (12).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   These details must be up to date and checked regularly, as Google may use them to send important notices about the account, the verification or the published apps.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (13).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Accept the terms and make the payment

* To complete the registration, review and accept the Google Play Console terms and conditions.
* Then make the one-off registration payment for the developer account, which costs USD 25.
*   Once you've accepted the terms and completed the payment, Google will continue with the registration and verification of the account.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (14).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

Once the registration is complete, the company or entity will have a developer account in Google Play Console.

From this account you can manage the club's Android apps, review the developer profile information and complete the steps needed for the app to be published on Google Play.

It's important to keep access to the account used during registration and to check Google's communications regularly, to avoid blocks, delays or problems during the publishing process.
