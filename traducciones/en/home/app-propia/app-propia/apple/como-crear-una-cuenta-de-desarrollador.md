# How to create a developer account

To publish and manage an app in the Apple ecosystem, you need an account in the **Apple Developer Program**.\
This account lets the company identify itself officially to Apple and complete the steps needed to work with iOS apps.

Below we explain how to create and register the account.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
Go to the Apple Developer portal

*   The first step is to go to the official Apple Developer portal using the following link:\
    <a href="https://developer.apple.com/" class="button primary">Go to Apple Developer</a><br>

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (17).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
*   If you don't have an account yet, you will need to create a new one.

    > **Important:** the Apple account must be created with personal information, not directly with the company's details. Later, when enrolling in the Apple Developer Program, you will be asked for the organisation's legal information.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Apply to join the Apple Developer Program

* Once the Apple account has been created, you need to apply to join the **Apple Developer Program**.
*   This process is not immediate. Apple will first review the account information and carry out an initial check. This review can take a few days.<br>

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (18).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Select the entity type

* After the initial approval, Apple will ask you which type of entity you want to register the account as.
*   In this case, select the option for: **Company or organisation**<br>

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (19).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Company information required

* When you select the company or organisation option, Apple shows a list of the information needed to continue.
* The details it may ask for include:
  * The company's **D-U-N-S** number.
  * The company's full legal name.
  * Address and tax details.
  * Name and job title of the person authorised to act on behalf of the company.
  * Company website.
  *   Contact phone number.<br>

      <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (20).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

#### D-U-N-S number

* The **D-U-N-S** number is a unique company identifier that Apple may ask for to verify the organisation.
* If the company or entity doesn't have this number yet, you'll need to request it before continuing with the account setup by clicking the following button:\
  <a href="https://cloud.email.informa.es/numero-duns" class="button primary">Request a D-U-N-S number</a>
* There are two options:
  * **Priority request:** you get the number    &#x20;immediately for a fee of €9 + VAT.
  * **Free request:** there is no cost, but the number    &#x20;takes 10 to 14 days to be issued.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (3).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Enter the legal name and D-U-N-S number

* Once you have the D-U-N-S number, Apple will ask for two main details:
  * **The company's full legal name**, exactly as it appears in the official records.
  *   **D-U-N-S number**, linked to that same company.<br>

      <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (21).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
* Before continuing, check that both details match.
* If the legal name you enter doesn't match the one linked to the D-U-N-S number, Apple may reject or delay the verification.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Complete the additional information

*   After entering the legal name and D-U-N-S number, you need to fill in the rest of the form with additional company information.

    Apple may ask for details such as:

    * The company's official website.
    * Contact phone number.
    * Confirmation that the person registering has the legal authority to sign contracts on behalf of the organisation.
    *   Verification of the email address linked to the account.<br>

        <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (22).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Review and confirm the information

* Before submitting the application, review all the details you've entered.
* Check in particular:
  * Company legal name.
  * D-U-N-S number.
  * Address and tax details.
  * Website.
  * Contact phone number.
  *   Email address linked to the account.<br>

      <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (23).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
* Once the application has been submitted, any mistake can delay approval of the registration.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Wait for Apple's verification

*   After you submit the application, Apple will review all the information provided.<br>

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (24).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
* This process can take several working days, as Apple validates both the company details and the applicant's identity.
* When the verification is complete, Apple will send an email to the account's main contact with instructions for the last registration step.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Sign the legal agreement and make the payment

* When Apple approves the application, you'll receive an email with instructions to access the developer portal.<br>

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (25).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

* From there you need to:
  1. Review and sign Apple's legal agreement.
  2. Pay the annual Apple Developer Program membership.
* According to the documentation, the membership costs **USD 99 per year**.
* Once this step is complete, the account is registered in the Apple Developer Program.<br>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

When the process is finished, the company will have an active developer account to work with apps in the Apple ecosystem.
