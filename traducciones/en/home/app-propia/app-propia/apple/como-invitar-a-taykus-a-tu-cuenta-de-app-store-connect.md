# How to invite Taykus to your App Store Connect account

So that we can help you set up or manage your iOS app, you need to invite a member of the Taykus team to your **App Store Connect** account.

On this platform, Apple lets you manage users, permissions and access for apps that are published or in the process of being published.

***

#### Download the invitation guide

You can download the guide with screenshots from the following link:

{% file src="../../.gitbook/assets/Guía de invitación a App Store Connect.pdf" %}

> **Important:** for security reasons, the file may be password-protected.\
> If you don't have the password, ask the Taykus team for it.

***

Once you've sent the invitation, the Taykus team can accept the access and carry on with the necessary management or setup.
