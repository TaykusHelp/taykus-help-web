# How to create a player

Having your players properly registered lets you check their information, history and contact details, and avoids duplicates in the club's database.

You can create a new player in two ways: from **Players** or from the **Calendar**, when making a booking. Both take you to the same profile.

## From Players

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Players

* Click **Players** in the left-hand menu.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (15).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Create the player

* Click <i class="fa-plus">:plus:</i>.
* The player profile opens for you to fill in their details.
* When you've filled in the details, click **Save**.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## From the Calendar

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Calendar

* Click **Calendar** in the left-hand menu.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Open the booking

* Click to create a new booking or open an existing one. The process is the same in both cases.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Create the player

* Select the **Player** field.
* Click **Create new**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (16).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> If you already have players created, the **Create new** option appears at the bottom of the drop-down list of players.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## The player profile

Either option takes you to this profile:

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (17).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The profile has lots of fields in case you want to keep complete information about the player, but not all of them are needed.

> **Important:** only these fields are required to save the profile:
>
> * **Name**
> * **Email**
> * **Customer type**
