# How to cancel a sale from the player's profile

You can cancel a player's sale directly from their profile, without having to look for it in **Sales › Lines**. From the profile, the whole sales line is cancelled.

Below are the steps to follow:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Open the player's profile

*   Go to the left-hand menu and select **Players**.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (92).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* Find the player using the table filters (for example, by name).
* Click their **Id** to open their profile.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Go to their purchases

*   Click the **Billing** tab and, in the drop-down, choose **Purchases**.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/ficha-facturacion-compras.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* The **Lines** tab opens, with all the player's sales.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Select the sale

*   Tick the circle on the left of the sale you want to cancel.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/ficha-compras-lineas.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* Click **Cancel**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Choose the cancel option

*   A drop-down opens. Choose **Cancel debts and refund payments**.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/ficha-compras-anular.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   A window opens asking you to confirm:

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/ficha-compras-anular-confirmar.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* Click **OK** to cancel it, or **Close** if you don't want to cancel it.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

> **Important:** if cancelling means refunding a cash or card payment, you need to have a cash register open. If it's a refund of an online payment, you don't.

You can also cancel sales from **Sales › Lines**. See [How to cancel a sale from Lines](../../../ventas/ventas/lineas/como-anular-una-venta-desde-lineas.md).
