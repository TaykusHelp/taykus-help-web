# How to assign a responsible customer to a player

The responsible customer is the person who takes care of another player profile: for example, a mother and her son, a couple or a group of friends. Each player can only have one responsible customer, but the same responsible customer can have several associated players.

> **Important:** the responsible customer is assigned from the associated person's profile, not from the responsible customer's. For example, to link a son with his mother, open the son's profile and choose the mother as **Responsible Customer**.

Below are the steps to follow:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to the player's profile

* Go to the left-hand menu and select **Players**.
* Find the player you want to associate using the table filters (for example, by name).
*   Click their **Id** to open their profile.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/jugadores-listado-id.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Edit the profile

* Click the <i class="fa-pen">:pen:</i> icon to edit the profile.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Choose the responsible customer

* Scroll down to the **Additional Contact Information** section.
*   In the **Responsible Customer** field, type any detail of the person: first name, last name, ID number, email…

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/cliente-responsable-campo.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   Select the person in the list of results.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/cliente-responsable-buscar.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   If they are not registered yet, click **Create New**, at the end of the list. The form to create a new player opens. To learn how to fill it in, see this article:

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/cliente-responsable-crear-nuevo.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

    <a href="como-crear-a-un-jugador.md" class="button primary">How to create a player</a>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Save

* Click **Save**.

> **Important:** when you save, if the associated profile has no email or phone, they are filled in with the responsible customer's, and everything sent to that profile will reach them.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
