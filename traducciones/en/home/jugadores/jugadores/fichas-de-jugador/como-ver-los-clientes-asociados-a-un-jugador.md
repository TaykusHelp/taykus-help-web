# How to see the customers associated with a player

From a responsible customer's profile you can check which players have them assigned as **Responsible Customer**.

Below are the steps to follow:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to the responsible customer's profile

* Go to the left-hand menu and select **Players**.
* Find the responsible customer using the table filters (for example, by name).
* Click their **Id** to open their profile.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Associates tab

*   Click the **Associates** tab, at the top of the profile. You'll see the list of players associated with this customer.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/pestana-asociados.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* To open an associate's profile, click their **Id**.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

> **Important:** the **Associates** tab only shows players in the responsible customer's profile. In an associated player's profile, their responsible customer appears in the **Responsible Customer** field.
