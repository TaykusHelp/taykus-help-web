# How player level works in Taykus

Player level ranks users according to their skill in the sport, such as padel or tennis. This way, matches can be organised more evenly and players can find opponents or partners of a similar level.

***

#### First step: get your level <a href="#primer-paso-nivelarse" id="primer-paso-nivelarse"></a>

For the system to assign an initial level to a player, the first step is to complete the levelling.

Levelling sets a starting point based on the player's experience and skill in the sport. From that initial level, the system adjusts the level over time based on competitive matches and the results recorded.

This first level is only an initial reference, so it isn't final. The more competitive matches the user plays, the more information the system has to adjust their level gradually.

To find out how to get your level, click the following button:

***

#### Player's initial level <a href="#nivel-inicial-del-jugador" id="nivel-inicial-del-jugador"></a>

When a player starts using Taykus, they may be assigned an initial level based on the club's settings or the information given when signing up.

This level is a starting point. From there, the level can be updated based on the player's activity and the results of the matches that count towards the calculation.

***

#### How the level can change <a href="#como-puede-cambiar-el-nivel" id="como-puede-cambiar-el-nivel"></a>

A player's level can change based on the results of competitive matches and other factors related to the players taking part.

Factors that can have an influence include:

* The match result.
* The opponents' level.
* The partner's level, in doubles matches.
* The reliability of the player's level.
* The number of matches played.

Winning against higher-level players can make your level go up more significantly. On the other hand, losing against lower-level players can make it drop more.

***

#### What level reliability means <a href="#que-es-la-fiabilidad-del-nivel" id="que-es-la-fiabilidad-del-nivel"></a>

Reliability shows how established a player's level is.

When a player has played few matches, their level can vary more, because the system doesn't yet have enough information to set it accurately.

As the player takes part in more competitive matches, reliability increases and the level becomes more stable. This means the changes after each match are smaller and more gradual.

***

#### Why the level can change differently for each player <a href="#por-que-el-nivel-puede-cambiar-de-forma-diferente-en-cada-jugador" id="por-que-el-nivel-puede-cambiar-de-forma-diferente-en-cada-jugador"></a>

After the same match, each player's level may change by a different amount. This is normal, as the system takes into account each participant's individual situation.

This can happen for several reasons:

* Each player may have a different reliability.
* Each player's initial level may be different.
* The system compares each player's level with that of their opponents and partners.
* Players with lower reliability can have bigger changes.

For this reason, two players who have won or lost the same match won't necessarily get exactly the same change in level.

***

#### Competitive and friendly matches <a href="#partidos-competitivos-y-partidos-amistosos" id="partidos-competitivos-y-partidos-amistosos"></a>

Only matches set as competitive affect the player's level.

If the match is set as friendly, the result doesn't change the participants' level. This option lets people play without the result affecting the player's ranking or progress.

***

#### When the level may not change <a href="#cuando-puede-no-cambiar-el-nivel" id="cuando-puede-no-cambiar-el-nivel"></a>

If a player's level doesn't change after a match, it may be for one of these reasons:

* The match was set as friendly.
* The result wasn't validated correctly.
* The score entered wasn't valid.
* The match didn't meet the conditions needed to affect the level.

***

#### Requirements for a valid score <a href="#requisitos-para-que-una-puntuacion-sea-valida" id="requisitos-para-que-una-puntuacion-sea-valida"></a>

For a result to count towards the level calculation, the score must be valid for the format of the sport.

In general, the result must meet these conditions:

* One player or team must have more points than the other.
* At least one of the players or teams must have reached 6 or 7 games.
* If neither player or team has reached 7 games, there must be a difference of at least 2 games. For example, 6-4 would be valid, but 6-5 would not.
* If one of the players or teams has reached 7 games, a 1-game difference is allowed. For example, 7-6 would be valid.

***

#### What happens if the match ends in a draw <a href="#que-ocurre-si-el-partido-termina-en-empate" id="que-ocurre-si-el-partido-termina-en-empate"></a>

If there's a draw, the level can also be affected.

If the teams have different levels, the system may adjust the levels to balance the ranking. For example, the team with the lower combined level may gain points, while the team with the higher combined level may lose them.

This helps the system keep a fairer and more balanced progression over time.
