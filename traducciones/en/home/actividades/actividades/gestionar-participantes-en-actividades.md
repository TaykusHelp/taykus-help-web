# Manage activity participants

The **Participants** section lets you see and manage everyone registered for an activity. From here you can review the information for each registration, add manual notes or export the full list of participants.

This gives the club clear control over attendees and access to the information collected during registration.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to the Participants section

* Go to the left-hand menu and open **Activities**.
* Click the ID of the activity you want to manage.
*   At the top, click the **Participants** tab.<br>

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (4).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### View and manage participants

In this section you can see the full list of people registered for the activity.

From here you can:

* Review each participant's information.
* Check the details collected on the registration form.
* Add **manual comments or notes** if you need to record extra information.

This keeps all the information about participants organised in one place.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Export the list of participants

If you need to work with the information outside the system, you can export the list of participants.

*   Click ID to select all the participants.

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (5).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   Select export and choose the download format.

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (6).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* The download then starts and you'll find the file in your downloads folder.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

This way, the club can easily view and manage all the registrations for the activity. The Participants section brings together the information collected during registration and also lets you export it to review or process it elsewhere when needed.
