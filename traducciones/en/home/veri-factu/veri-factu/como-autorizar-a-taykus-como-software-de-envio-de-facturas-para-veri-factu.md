# How to authorise Taykus as invoice submission software for Veri\*FACTU

Authorising Taykus with the Spanish Tax Agency (Agencia Tributaria) is a mandatory step so that the system can automatically send your invoices to Veri\*FACTU and comply with the regulations. It's a simple process, but essential: without this authorisation, invoices cannot be reported and submission is blocked. In this guide you'll see, step by step, how to complete the authorisation on the Tax Agency website and get your account ready to work with VeriFactu without any hassle.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to the Tax Agency's online portal (Sede Electrónica)

* Click the following link to open it.\
  <a href="https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/procedimientoini/ZP01.shtml" class="button primary">Go to the Tax Agency's online portal</a>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Identify yourself

*   You can identify yourself with **Cl@ve Móvil** or with a **digital certificate or electronic ID card (DNI electrónico)**.<br>

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Alta de poder mediante identificación electrónica (register a power of attorney with electronic ID)

*   Click **Alta de poder mediante identificación electrónica.**

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   On the next screen, click **Alta de poder para trámites tributarios específicos.**<br>

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/2.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Alta de poder para trámites tributarios específicos (power of attorney for specific tax procedures)

*   When you log in with your digital certificate, this screen for the grantor (poderdante) appears.

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image001.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>


*   Click the :heavy\_plus\_sign: button next to **Impuestos, tasas y prestaciones patrimoniales.**

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image002.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* A drop-down opens.
*   Click the :heavy\_plus\_sign: button next to **IVA** (VAT).<br>

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image003.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>


* Scroll down until you find :heavy\_plus\_sign: **Sistemas informáticos de facturación y veri\*factu.**
*   Select everything.<br>

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image007.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* Once done, enter the tax ID (NIF) of the representative, that is, Taykus's: **B72652456.**
* Click Aceptar (Accept).
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
