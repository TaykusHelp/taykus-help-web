# How to issue an invoice from Sales > Lines

Managing sales and then invoicing them is one of the most used parts of the program. To do this, you need to know how to find a sale that has already been recorded, open its details and issue an invoice with the right tax details.

The following video shows how to do this:

### Explainer video

{% embed url="https://youtu.be/dW3OHLSIm6s" %}

In this article we explain how to issue an invoice from Sales > Lines and the steps to follow:

### Before you start

Before issuing the invoice, check that the following details have been created in:

* **Settings > Sales > Companies**
* **Settings > Sales > Customer companies**

To find out how, you can read the following articles:

* [How to set up your company details](https://app.gitbook.com/s/V0h7jTfpeuZY1RmPEtNK/configuracion/ventas/empresas/como-configurar-los-datos-de-empresa "mention")
* [How to create customer companies in the system](https://app.gitbook.com/s/V0h7jTfpeuZY1RmPEtNK/configuracion/ventas/empresas-clientes/como-crear-empresas-cliente-en-el-sistema "mention")

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Sales - Lines

* In the side menu, go to **Sales**.
* Click **Lines**.
*   Find the sales line you want to issue the invoice from.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (6).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Issue the invoice

* Once you've found the line, click the **ID**.
*   In the sale, press the **Issue invoice** button.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (7).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* A window with the billing details opens.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Fill in the invoice details

In the **Issuer** block:

* Select the **issuing company** in the **Company** field.
* The system automatically fills in the linked details, such as:
  * Name
  * Tax ID (CIF)
  * Email
  * Phone

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (8).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

In the **Customer** block, you can choose between two options:

**Individual**

If the invoice is for a private individual:

* Leave the **Individual** option ticked.
* Check or fill in the customer's details:
  * Full name
  * ID number (DNI)
  * Email
  * Phone
  * Address

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (9).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Company**

If the invoice is for a company:

* Tick the **Company** option.
* In the **Customer company** field, select the company.
* The system automatically fills in that company's tax details.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (10).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>


{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Save the invoice

* When all the details have been checked, press the **save** icon.
* The system generates the invoice linked to that sale.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

Once all the steps above are complete and the billing details have been checked, the invoice is issued correctly in the system. Always remember to check that both the issuer's and the customer's details are correct before saving, to avoid invoicing errors.
