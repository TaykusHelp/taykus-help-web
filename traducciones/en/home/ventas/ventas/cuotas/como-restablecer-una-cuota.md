# How to reset a fee

Resetting a fee undoes the issue: the fee goes back to how it was before it was issued. It's useful if you issued it by mistake or need to change its price or due date, or skip it, because an issued fee can only be paid or reset.

Below are the steps to follow:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to the fees

* Go to the left-hand menu and select **Sales**.
* In the drop-down, choose **Fees**.
* Click the fee's **Id**.
* Go to the **Calendar** tab.

> You can also do it from the player's profile or from an academy group. To find out how, see this article:
>
> <a href="como-ver-las-cuotas.md" class="button primary">How to view fees</a>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Select the fee

* Tick the circle of the month's fee you want to change, in the player's row.
* You can select several at once: the circle next to a player's name selects all their fees, and the circle next to a month selects every player's fee for that month. The action applies to all the ones you select.
*   Click **Actions**, at the top left.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/cuotas-acciones-emitida.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Reset

* In the **Actions** menu, choose **Reset**.
*   Click **OK** to confirm.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/cuotas-restablecer.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

The pending sales line disappears, both from Sales › Lines and from the player's Customer Area. From then on, the fee has all its actions available again: pay, issue, change the price, skip or change the due date.
