# How to remove a customer from a fee

To remove a customer from a fee, or from a membership, change their end date on the fee. From that date their charges stop being issued and, if the fee is a membership, they move to the cancellation customer type.

Below are the steps to follow:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to the fee

* Go to the left-hand menu and select **Sales**.
*   In the drop-down, choose **Fees**.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/cuotas-listado.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* Click the fee's **Id**.
* Go to the **Players** tab.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Change the end date

*   Find the player in the list.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/cuotas-baja-jugadores.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* Edit their **End** date and set the day you want them to leave.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

> **Important:** only that player's date changes. The rest of the players on the fee stay the same.

To find out more about memberships, see [What a membership is and how it works](que-es-una-membresia-y-como-funciona.md).
