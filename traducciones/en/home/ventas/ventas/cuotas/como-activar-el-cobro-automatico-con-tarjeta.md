# How to turn on automatic card payment

If a player has a saved card, you can turn on automatic payment for their fee: when the due date arrives, the fee is charged automatically to that card. You can turn it on when you add the player to the fee or later, from the fee's player list.

> **Important:** automatic payment can only be turned on if the customer has a saved card. If an automatic payment fails, the fee stays as a debt pending payment in [Sales › Debts](../deudas/README.md).

### Before you start: how customers save their card

Customers can save their card from their customer area in two ways:

*   **When paying a fee online.** If the fee is pending payment, it appears in **My account › Pending Sales** so they can pay it. If they sign up for it online, it's added to the cart. In both cases, when paying by card, the system requires them to save it.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/cuotas-tarjeta-ventas-pendientes.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/cuotas-tarjeta-guardar.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   **From their profile, without paying anything.** In **My account › My profile › Payment methods**, with the **Add card** button. Nothing is charged at that moment.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/cuotas-tarjeta-mi-perfil.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/cuotas-tarjeta-anadir-tarjeta.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Below are the steps to follow:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to the fee

* Go to the left-hand menu and select **Sales**.
* In the drop-down, choose **Fees**.
* Click the fee's **ID**.
* Open the **Players** tab.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Turn it on when adding a player

* Click **+** to add a player to the fee.
* Fill in the details: **Customer**, **Start**, **End**, **Due day** and **Description**.
*   Tick the **Automatic Card Payment** box.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/cuotas-cobro-tarjeta-anadir.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* Click **Save**.
*   If the customer doesn't have a saved card, a red warning appears and you can't turn on automatic payment:<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/cuotas-cobro-tarjeta-sin-tarjeta.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Turn it on for a player already in the fee

*   In the fee's player list, the **Card** column shows whether the customer has a saved card, and the **Automatic Card Payment** column shows whether automatic payment is on.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/cuotas-cobro-tarjeta-listado.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   If the player has a card, click their cell in the **Automatic Card Payment** column and tick the box. It's saved automatically.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/cuotas-cobro-tarjeta-activar.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
