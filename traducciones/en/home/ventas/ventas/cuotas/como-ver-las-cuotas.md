# How to view fees

You can check fees in three ways: by fee, to see everyone who has it; by player, to see all of a person's fees; or from an academy group.

## From Sales: everyone with a fee

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Fees

*   Go to the left-hand menu, select **Sales** and, in the drop-down, choose **Fees**. You'll see the list of all the club's fees.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/cuotas-listado.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Open the fee's calendar

* Click the fee's **Id**.
*   Go to the **Calendar** tab. You'll see one row for each person who has that fee and one column for each month, with the amount of each receipt.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/cuotas-calendario.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## From the player's profile: all their fees

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to the profile

* Go to the left-hand menu and select **Players**.
* Find the player and click their **Id**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Open their fees

*   Click the **Billing** tab and, in the drop-down, choose **Fees**. You'll see all of that player's fees, month by month.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/cuotas-ficha-jugador.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## What you can do with a fee

From any of these screens, tick the circle of a month's fee and click **Actions**. You can select several at once:

*   The circle next to a player's name selects all their fees.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/cuotas-seleccionar-jugador.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   The circle next to a month selects every player's fee for that month.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/cuotas-seleccionar-mes.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Depending on the fee's status, you'll have different actions:

* If the fee **hasn't been issued**, you can pay it, issue it, change its price, skip it or change its due date.
* If the fee **has already been issued**, you can only pay it or reset it.

## What each colour means

* **Blue:** not issued.
* **Orange:** issued, pending payment.
* **Green:** paid.
* **Grey:** skipped.
