# How to automate fee issuing

If you turn on automatic issuing for a fee, you no longer have to issue it by hand every month from the fee calendar: the system issues it by itself.

> **Important:** the fee is issued automatically **the day after its due date, at 00:00**. For example, if it's due on the 1st, it's issued on the 2nd at 00:00.

Below are the steps to follow:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Open the fee

* Click the <i class="fa-gear" style="color:blue;">:gear:</i> icon in the top right corner and go to **Settings**.
* Click **Sales**. The **Products** tab opens directly.
* In the **Category** column, select **Fee** and click the fee's **Id**.
* Click the <i class="fa-pen">:pen:</i> icon to edit it.

> If the fee doesn't exist yet, you can fill in these fields when you create it. See [How to create a Fee product](../../../configuracion/configuracion/ventas/productos/como-crear-un-producto-tipo-cuota.md).
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Turn on automatic issuing

*   In the **Automatic Fees** block, tick **Automatic Issue**.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/cuotas-automaticas-campos.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* Click **Save**.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

Once issued, the fee is pending payment and the customer sees it in their Customer Area. To collect it automatically too, see [How to turn on automatic card payment](como-activar-el-cobro-automatico-con-tarjeta.md).
