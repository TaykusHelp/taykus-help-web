# How to pay a fee

You can charge a fee from the fees calendar, whether it's been issued or not. When you pay it, the system takes you to the POS to charge it like a normal sale.

Below are the steps to follow:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to the fees

* Go to the left-hand menu and select **Sales**.
* In the drop-down, choose **Fees**.
* Click the fee's **Id**.
* Go to the **Calendar** tab.

> You can also do it from the player's profile or from an academy group. To find out how, see this article:
>
> <a href="como-ver-las-cuotas.md" class="button primary">How to view fees</a>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Select the fee

* Tick the circle of the month's fee you want to pay, in the player's row.
* You can select several at once: the circle next to a player's name selects all their fees, and the circle next to a month selects every player's fee for that month. The action applies to all the ones you select.
*   Click **Actions**, at the top left.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/cuotas-acciones.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Pay

* In the **Actions** menu, choose **Pay**.
*   Click **OK** to confirm.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/cuotas-pagar.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> **Important:** if you've selected fees that haven't been issued yet, they'll be issued when you pay them.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Charge at the POS

* The POS opens with the fee. Charge it with the customer's preferred payment method.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
