# Debts pending direct debit

In **Sales › Debts**, the **Pending Direct Debit** tab shows the debts that will be collected by direct debit and are still waiting to be included in a remittance. It works just like the **Pending Payment** tab: you can collect them, cancel them or let the customer know the payment will be taken from their bank account.

Below are the steps to follow:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to debts pending direct debit

* Go to the left-hand menu and select **Sales**.
* In the drop-down, choose **Debts**.
*   Open the **Pending Direct Debit** tab.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/deudas-pendiente-domiciliacion.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Choose what to do

Tick the box to the left of the debts you want and click the button you need:

* **Open POS:** to collect them. See [How to collect a debt](como-cobrar-una-deuda.md).
* **Cancel:** to cancel them. See [How to cancel a debt](como-anular-una-deuda.md).
* **Send Direct Debit Email:** to let the customer know that the payment will be taken from their bank account in the next few days, so they have enough funds. One email is sent for each selected debt, just like in [How to send a debts email](como-enviar-un-email-de-deudas.md).
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

> **Important:** the direct debit email isn't a debt reminder, it's a notice of the upcoming bank charge. If you want to chase a pending payment, use the **Pending Payment** tab and the **Send Debts Email** button.

To include these debts in a remittance, see [Generating the Q19 (direct debit of bank receipts)](../q19/generacion-del-q19-domiciliacion-de-recibos-bancarios.md).

The text of this email is the **Direct debit notification** template, which you can change from **Communications › Email Templates**. See [Accessing email templates](https://taykus-help-center.gitbook.io/taykushelpcenter/home/comunicaciones/comunicaciones/acceso-a-plantillas-de-emails).
