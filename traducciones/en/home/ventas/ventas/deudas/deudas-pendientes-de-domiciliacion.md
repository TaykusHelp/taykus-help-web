# Debts pending direct debit

In **Sales › Debts**, the **Pending Direct Debit** tab shows the debts that will be collected by direct debit. It works just like the **Pending Payment** tab: you can collect them, cancel them or notify the customer by email.

Below are the steps to follow:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to debts pending direct debit

* Go to the left-hand menu and select **Sales**.
* In the drop-down, choose **Debts**.
*   Open the **Pending Direct Debit** tab.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/deudas-pendiente-domiciliacion.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Choose what to do

Tick the box to the left of the debts you want and click the button you need:

* **Open POS:** to collect them. See [How to collect a debt](como-cobrar-una-deuda.md).
* **Cancel:** to cancel them. See [How to cancel a debt](como-anular-una-deuda.md).
* **Send Direct Debit Email:** to send the customer an email. One email is sent for each selected debt, just like in [How to send a debts email](como-enviar-un-email-de-deudas.md).
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

The text of this email is the **Direct debit notification** template, which you can change from **Communications › Email Templates**. See [Accessing email templates](https://taykus-help-center.gitbook.io/taykushelpcenter/home/comunicaciones/comunicaciones/acceso-a-plantillas-de-emails).
