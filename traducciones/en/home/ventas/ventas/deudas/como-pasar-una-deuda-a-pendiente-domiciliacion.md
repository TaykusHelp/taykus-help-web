# How to move a debt from Pending Payment to Pending Direct Debit (and back)

If a debt in **Pending Payment** is going to be collected by direct debit, you can move it to **Pending Direct Debit** so it's included in the next remittance. You can also make the change the other way round. The change is made from the POS.

Below are the steps to follow:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Debts

* Go to the left-hand menu and select **Sales**.
* In the drop-down, choose **Debts**.
* Open the tab where the debt is now: **Pending Payment** or **Pending Direct Debit**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Open the debt in the POS

* Tick the box to the left of the debt.
*   Click **Open POS**.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/deudas-cambiar-desde-pago.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   It works the same way from the **Pending Direct Debit** tab:<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/deudas-cambiar-desde-domiciliacion.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Change the status

On the right-hand side of the POS, below the total, click the option you need:

*   **Pending Direct Debit**, to move it to direct debit.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/deudas-cambiar-tpv-a-domiciliacion.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   **Pending Payment**, to move it back to pending payment.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/deudas-cambiar-tpv-a-pago.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* The screen flickers for a moment: the change is done.
* Click **Exit**, at the bottom right, to leave the POS.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Check the change

* Go back to **Sales › Debts**.
* The debt now appears in the other tab: in **Pending Direct Debit** if you moved it to direct debit, or in **Pending Payment** if you moved it back.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

> **Important:** you can also do it from **Sales › Lines**: tick the line, click **Open POS** and follow step 3.

To learn more about direct debit debts, see [Debts pending direct debit](deudas-pendientes-de-domiciliacion.md).
