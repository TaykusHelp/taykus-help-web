# Lines

**Sales › Lines** lists everything the club generates, whether it's been paid or not: bookings, fees, products, voucher top-ups… Each sale appears as a line.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/lineas-listado.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can filter by any column and download the list with the download button. From this screen you can:

* [Collect a sale](como-cobrar-una-venta-desde-lineas.md) by opening it in the POS.
* [Cancel a sale](como-anular-una-venta-desde-lineas.md).
* [Cancel a voucher top-up](como-anular-una-recarga-de-bono.md).
* Issue an invoice by clicking the line's **ID**. See [How to issue an invoice from Sales > Lines](../facturas/como-emitir-una-factura-desde-ventas-greater-than-lineas.md).

When a sale is cancelled, two coloured lines appear in the list: in light pink, the refund (with a negative amount), and in darker pink, the cancelled sale.

> **Important:** you need to have a cash register open to collect or cancel. See [How to open the cash register](../cajas/como-abrir-la-caja.md).
