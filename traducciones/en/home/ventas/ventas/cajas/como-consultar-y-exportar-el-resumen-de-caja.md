# How to view and export the cash register summary

In the **Summary** tab of **Sales › Cash registers** you can see how much has been collected between two dates, by payment method and by product type, and download it to Excel. This tab is for viewing only: cash register operations are done from **Movements**.

Below are the steps to follow:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to the summary

* Go to the left-hand menu and select **Sales**.
* In the drop-down, choose **Cash registers**.
* The **Summary** tab opens straight away.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Choose the dates and cash registers

* In **Filters**, enter the **From** and **To** dates. The **To** day isn't included: to see a single day, enter that day in **From** and the following day in **To**. For example, to see 27/10, enter 27/10 to 28/10.
*   Below, tick the cash registers you want to include. You can tick one or several: the ticked ones show a green check.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/cajas-resumen.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### View the summary

* The **Summary** table shows, for each payment method, the **Payments**, **Manual Cash In**, **Manual Cash Out**, **Cash Out on Close** and **Discrepancies on Close**, with the total at the bottom. On the right, the **Income** chart shows the split by payment method.
*   Further down, the **Summary by product type** table shows what was collected for each product type (bookings, fees, equipment…) and payment method, with the **Amount by product type** chart.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/cajas-resumen-tipo-producto.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Export to Excel

*   Click **Export summary**. An Excel file is downloaded with the **Summary** table by payment method.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/cajas-resumen-excel.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
