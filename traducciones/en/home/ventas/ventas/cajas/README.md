# Cash registers

**Sales › Cash registers** is where you keep track of the money coming in and going out of the club. The screen has two tabs:

* **Summary:** totals collected by payment method and by product type, with charts, between the dates you choose. Here you can only view the data and export it to Excel. See [How to view and export the cash register summary](como-consultar-y-exportar-el-resumen-de-caja.md).
* **Movements:** the list of all cash register operations (payments, cash in, cash out…). This is where you carry out cash register operations with the **Count**, **Cash out**, **Cash in** and **Close** buttons.
