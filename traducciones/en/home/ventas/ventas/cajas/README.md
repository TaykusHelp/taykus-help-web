# Cash registers

{% tabs %}
{% tab title="Open" %}
## How to open the cash register

The **Cash registers** section lets you keep track of the income recorded at the club, check the transactions made and review the summary of the different payment methods.

From this section you can also **open a cash register**, entering the details needed to start recording transactions correctly during the day.

In this article we'll see how to go to **Sales > Cash registers** and how to open the cash register step by step.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Cash registers

* Go to the left-hand column and select Sales.
* A drop-down opens. Select Cash registers.\
  ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (2).png>)
*   The cash register summary opens. Click the **Transactions** tab.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (3).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Open the cash register

*   Click **Open.**<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (4).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   A window appears to confirm the opening of the main cash register.

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (5).png" alt="" width="366"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>


* This window shows the following fields:
  * **Cash:** enter the actual amount of cash the cash register starts with.
  * **You should have:** below the cash field, the system shows the amount that should be in the cash register according to the previous closing.\
    This amount is the cash that was recorded when the cash register was closed the day before.
  * **Notes:** this field lets you add any comment or explanation about the opening. For example, if the actual cash doesn't match the expected amount.
  * **Cancel:** closes the window without opening the cash register.
  * **OK:** confirms the opening with the details entered.

> **Note:** If the amount shown in “You should have” doesn't match the actual cash in the cash register, check the amount before opening it and add a note explaining the difference if needed.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

Once you've filled in the form and pressed **OK**, the cash register is open and you can start recording transactions linked to it.

From this same screen you can check the summary of payment methods, the income chart, filter by date and review the cash register transactions whenever you need.
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Count" %}
## How to do a cash count

A cash count lets you check the money in the cash register at a specific moment, without closing the day.

This option is useful for checking whether the actual cash matches the amount the system calculates there should be according to the transactions recorded. This way, the club can spot any differences, review the status of the cash register and keep a record of the count before carrying on.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Cash registers

* Go to the left-hand column and select Sales.
* A drop-down opens. Select Cash registers.\
  <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (2).png" alt="" data-size="original">
*   The cash register summary opens. Click the **Transactions** tab.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Do the count

* Click **Count**.\
  ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (26).png>)
*   This doesn't close the cash register; it lets you check the cash available at that moment.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (28).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

    *   **Cash**

        In the **Cash** field, you can enter the total amount of cash in the cash register directly.

        Below the field, you'll see the amount the system calculates you should have, according to the transactions recorded up to that point.

        This lets you compare the actual amount counted with the amount the system expects.
    *   **Count**

        You can also fill in the **Count** section, entering the actual number of coins and notes in the cash register.

        To do this, enter how many of each denomination you have: cents, coins and notes. The system calculates the total cash automatically.

        * The **Clear** button deletes the amounts entered in the count so you can start again.
    *   **Card**

        In the **Card** field you can enter the total amount taken by card during the day.

        * Below the field, you'll see the amount the system calculates you **should have** in card payments, according to the transactions recorded.
    *   **Notes**

        The **Notes** field lets you add any explanation about the count, especially if there are differences between the actual amounts and the amounts the system expects.
    *   **Confirm the count**

        Once you've checked the details, press **OK** to confirm.

        If you don't want to continue, you can press **Cancel** or close the window with the **X** at the top.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

Once the count is confirmed, the system saves the information entered without closing the cash register.

This way, the club can see the real status of the cash register at that moment, check any differences between the cash counted and the expected amount, and keep better control over the transactions recorded during the day.
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Cash in or out" %}
## How to record a cash in or cash out

Cash in and cash out let you record manual cash register transactions that aren't linked to sales, such as topping up the float or small payments. Each operation is reflected in the closing and the reports, ensuring control and traceability.

Here's how to do it step by step:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Cash registers

* Go to the left-hand column and select Sales.
* A drop-down opens. Select Cash registers.\
  ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (24).png>)
*   The cash register summary opens. Click the **Transactions** tab.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (25).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Cash in / Cash out

* Click Cash out / Cash in.\
  ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (26).png>)
* Both options open the following form:\
  ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (27).png>)
* Fill it in as follows:
  * **Cash:** The amount of cash being taken out of / put into the cash register.
  * **Comments:** The reason for the cash out / cash in.
* Press OK to save.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

With this procedure, the transaction is recorded in the cash register and can be checked later in the cash register transactions, the closing and the available reports. This way, the club keeps clear control of manual cash in and cash out, keeping them separate from sales made in the system.

Before saving, we recommend checking that the amount and the comment are correct, as this information will justify the transaction if there's an internal review.
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Close" %}
## How to close the cash register

Closing the cash register ends the working day and records the cash available at the end of the day.

In this process you can enter how much money is left in the cash register, check any differences and add notes if needed. This figure is important, as it's used as the reference for the next opening.

In this article we'll see how to close the cash register from **Sales > Cash registers** and what information you need to fill in before confirming the closing.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Cash registers

* Go to the left-hand column and select Sales.
* A drop-down opens. Select Cash registers.\
  <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (2).png" alt="" data-size="original">
*   The cash register summary opens. Click the **Transactions** tab.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Close the cash register

* Click **Close**.\
  ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (26).png>)
*   When you press **Close**, a window appears to record the closing of the main cash register.

    In this window you can enter the cash and card payments recorded at the end of the day.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

    *   **Cash**

        In the **Cash** field you can enter the total amount of cash in the cash register at closing directly.

        Below the field, you'll see the amount the system calculates you **should have**, according to the transactions recorded during the day.
    *   **Count**

        You can also fill in the **Count** section, entering the actual number of coins and notes in the cash register.

        To do this, enter how many of each denomination you have: cents, coins and notes. The system calculates the total cash automatically.

        * The **Clear** button deletes the amounts entered in the count so you can start again.
    *   **How much do you want to leave**

        In the **How much do you want to leave** field, enter the amount of cash that will stay in the cash register for the next day.

        * This amount will appear as the reference at the next opening, in the **“You should have”** section.
    *   **Card**

        In the **Card** field you can enter the total amount taken by card during the day.

        * Below the field, you'll see the amount the system calculates you **should have** in card payments, according to the transactions recorded.
    *   **Notes**

        The **Notes** field lets you add any explanation about the closing, especially if there are differences between the actual amounts and the amounts the system expects.
    *   **Confirm the closing**

        Once you've checked the details, press **OK** to confirm the closing.

        If you don't want to close the cash register, you can press **Cancel** or close the window with the **X** at the top.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

Once you've filled in the details and pressed **OK**, the cash register is closed and the system saves the cash entered as the final amount for the day.

This amount will appear as the reference at the next opening, in the **“You should have”** section, so you can check whether the actual cash matches what was recorded.
{% endtab %}
