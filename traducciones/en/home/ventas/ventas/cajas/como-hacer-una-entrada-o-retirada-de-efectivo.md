# How to record a cash in or cash out

Cash in and cash out let you record manual cash register transactions that aren't linked to sales, such as topping up the float or small payments. Each operation is reflected in the closing and the reports, ensuring control and traceability.

### Explainer video



Here's how to do it step by step:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Cash registers

* Go to the left-hand column and select Sales.
* A drop-down opens. Select Cash registers.\
  ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (24).png>)
*   The cash register summary opens. Click the **Transactions** tab.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (25).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Cash in / Cash out

* Click Cash out / Cash in.\
  ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (26).png>)
* Both options open the following form:\
  ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (27).png>)
* Fill it in as follows:
  * **Cash:** The amount of cash being taken out of / put into the cash register.
  * **Comments:** The reason for the cash out / cash in.
* Press OK to save.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

With this procedure, the transaction is recorded in the cash register and can be checked later in the cash register transactions, the closing and the available reports. This way, the club keeps clear control of manual cash in and cash out, keeping them separate from sales made in the system.

Before saving, we recommend checking that the amount and the comment are correct, as this information will justify the transaction if there's an internal review.
