# How to remove stock

In this module you can easily remove products from the inventory. You do it from **Manual Entry**: choose the product and enter, as a negative number, how many units you want to remove.

This helps keep your stock up to date and avoids inventory errors.

To do this, just follow these simple steps:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Manual Entry

* Go to the left-hand column and select **Warehouse**.
* In the drop-down, click **Manual Entry**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Remove the quantity

* In the top left corner, select **Add Entry**.
*   The following form opens, which you need to fill in as follows:<br>

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

    * **Qty:** The quantity you want to remove, entered as a **NEGATIVE** number by adding a minus sign in front.
    * **Search Product:** When you select it, a list of all the products with stock control opens.
* Click Add to save your changes.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

Once the manual entry is saved, the system updates the product's stock immediately.\
The adjustment is reflected in the inventory and can be checked or corrected later if needed.
