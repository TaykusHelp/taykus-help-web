# How matches work in the calendar

When you create an open match, it doesn't take up a real court until it's full. This way the club doesn't lose availability because of matches that don't have all their players yet.

## The "Matches" virtual court

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (36).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

When you create an open match, it's first placed on a virtual court called **Matches**. It isn't a physical court at the club: it's a temporary space where the match waits while players sign up.

## When the match is full

As soon as the match has all its players, the system automatically moves it to a real court that's free at that time.

## If the real courts fill up first

If all the real courts in that time slot are taken and the match is still not full, the system cancels it automatically. This avoids overlaps in the calendar.

Thanks to this, the club can encourage players to create matches without affecting the real availability of its courts.
