# Match types: competitive, friendly and gender

When you create a match, you choose whether it's competitive or friendly, which gender it's for and the players' level. This way each player knows what kind of match it is and whether they can join. Matches can be created by the club from the panel, or by players from the Taykus app and the customer area.

## Competitive or friendly

* **Competitive:** the result counts. Once it's recorded and validated, Taykus adjusts each player's level based on the result and the level of their partners and opponents.
* **Friendly:** it's played the same way, but the result doesn't change anyone's level. It's useful for playing without pressure or with players of very different levels.

In the club panel you choose it with the **Modifies Levels** checkbox: ticked means competitive and unticked means friendly. It's ticked by default.

> **Important:** if a player asks why their level hasn't changed after a match, first check whether the match was friendly.

## Gender

* **Open:** anyone can join, regardless of gender.
* **Male:** only people identified as male can join.
* **Female:** only people identified as female can join.
* **Mixed:** the match is **2 men and 2 women**. Once two players of one gender have joined, the remaining places can only be taken by people of the other gender. That's why there can't be, for example, 3 men and 1 woman.

When a player creates the match from the app or the customer area, they see **Open**, **Mixed** and their own gender.

> **Important:** "Mixed" doesn't mean "any gender", it means 2 and 2. If you want another combination (3 men and 1 woman, 3 women and 1 man…) or gender doesn't matter, choose **Open**.

## Level

The **minimum and maximum level** sets which players can join.

* **From the club panel:** it's optional. If you leave it empty, the level is set by the first player who joins.
* **From the app or the customer area:** the slider starts with a range around the player's level, and they can move it freely.
* **After the match is created:** only the club can change the range, from the panel. The player can't change it from the app or the customer area. See [How to change the level of a match](como-cambiar-el-nivel-de-un-partido.md).

To find out how the level is calculated, see the article **How player level works in Taykus**, in the Players module.
