# How to close a court for a few hours

If you only need to close a court for a few hours (for example, the morning, the afternoon or a while in the middle of the day), you don't need to close it for the whole day. There are two ways to do it from the calendar:

* **Change its opening hours** for that day or those days.
* **Block those hours with a booking** with no players.

> **Important:** it's done court by court. If you need to close several courts, repeat the process for each one.

## Option 1: change the opening hours

Below are the steps to follow:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Calendar

*   Go to the left-hand menu and select **Calendar**.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (65).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   Go to the day you want to change. You can choose it in the calendar at the top.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (38).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Open the court's hours

* Click the court's name. The court's **Opening Hours** window opens.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Change the hours

*   Change the **Start** and **End** to leave open only the hours you want.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/cerrar-horas-apertura.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

    * **Close the afternoon:** leave only the morning. For example, from 09:00 to 14:00.
    * **Close the morning:** leave only the afternoon. For example, from 16:00 to 22:30.
    * **Close for a while in the middle of the day:** create two time slots with **+**. For example, one from 09:00 to 14:00 and another from 16:00 to 22:30, to close from 14:00 to 16:00.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Choose the days and save

* In **Frequency**, tick **Today** if it's just for that day, or fill in **From** and **To** if it's several days in a row.
* Click **Save Changes**.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

To go back to the normal schedule, see [How to open a court](como-abrir-una-pista.md).

## Option 2: block the hours with a booking

You can also create a booking on that court **without adding any players** and set it to the hours you want to close. That way nobody can book that slot. To find out how to create a booking, see [How to create a booking](../reservas/como-crear-una-reserva.md).

> If the closure is for many days or for the whole club, it's better to change the schedule from Settings. See [How to close the club between two dates](https://app.gitbook.com/s/V0h7jTfpeuZY1RmPEtNK/configuracion/reservas/horarios/como-cerrar-el-club-entre-dos-fechas).
