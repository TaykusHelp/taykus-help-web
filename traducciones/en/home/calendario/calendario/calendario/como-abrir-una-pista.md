# How to open a court

If you've closed a court with **Close Whole Day**, you can open it again. Since the closed court disappears from the calendar on that day, you have to do it from a day when it does appear, choosing a period that includes the closed day.

For example, if the court is closed on the 17th, go to the calendar for the 16th and choose the period from the 16th to the 18th.

Below are the steps to follow:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Calendar

*   Go to the left-hand menu and select **Calendar**.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (65).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   Go to the day before the day the court is closed. You can choose the day in the calendar at the top.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (38).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Open the court's hours

* Click the court's name. The court's **Opening Hours** window opens.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Choose the period

*   In **Frequency**, leave **Today** unticked and fill in the dates:

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/abrir-pista-restaurar.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

    * **From:** the day before the closed day.
    * **To:** the day after the closed day.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Restore the schedule

* Click **Restore Default**.
* The court gets its normal schedule back and appears in the calendar again.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

> **Important:** only the selected court is opened. If you closed several courts that day, repeat the process for each of them.

To find out how to close a court, see [How to close a court](como-cerrar-una-pista.md).
