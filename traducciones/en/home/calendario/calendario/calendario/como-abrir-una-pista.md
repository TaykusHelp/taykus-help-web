# How to open a court

If you've closed a court with **Close Whole Day**, you can open it again. Since the closed court disappears from the calendar, you have to do it from a day when it does appear (the last open day before the closure or the first one after it), choosing a period that includes all the closed days.

For example:

* If the court is closed on the 17th, go to the calendar for the 16th (or the 18th) and choose the period from the 16th to the 18th.
* If the court is closed on the 16th and 17th, it doesn't appear on the 16th either. Go to the calendar for the 15th (or the 18th) and choose the period from the 15th to the 18th.

Below are the steps to follow:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Calendar

*   Go to the left-hand menu and select **Calendar**.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (65).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   Go to the day before or the day after the day the court is closed. You can choose the day in the calendar at the top.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (38).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   Check that the court appears on that day. If it doesn't, it's closed that day too: go back (or forward) one more day until you find the first day when the court does appear.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Open the court's hours

* Click the court's name. The court's **Opening Hours** window opens.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Choose the period

*   In **Frequency**, leave **Today** unticked and fill in the dates:

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/abrir-pista-restaurar.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

    * **From:** the day before the first closed day.
    * **To:** the day after the last closed day.

    > **Important:** if the court is closed for several days and you only want to open the last one, put that day in **From**. For example, if it's closed on the 16th and 17th and you only want to open the 17th, go to the 15th and choose the period from the 17th to the 18th. That way the 16th stays closed.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Restore the schedule

* Click **Restore Default**.
* The court gets its normal schedule back and appears in the calendar again.

> **Important:** **Restore Default** applies to every day in the period. If the day before or the day after had a special schedule, they also go back to their normal schedule. Check them afterwards and, if needed, set their schedule again.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

> **Important:** only the selected court is opened. If you closed several courts that day, repeat the process for each of them.

To find out how to close a court, see [How to close a court](como-cerrar-una-pista.md).
