# What each icon means

The icons and colours shown on bookings let you see important information at a glance, such as the booking status, where it was made or certain related actions. In this article we explain what each one means so you can read them quickly and easily.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (106).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### Calendar icons

<i class="fa-bolt">:bolt:</i> **Lightning bolt**\
This icon means that one or more players in the booking have an outstanding debt from a previous day.

<i class="fa-credit-card">:credit-card:</i> **Card**\
This icon means the player has chosen the split or shared payment option.

<i class="fa-memo">:memo:</i> **Torn ticket**\
This icon means that **one or more players in the booking still have their share to pay**.

<img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Sin fondo.jpg" alt="" data-size="line"> **TAYKUS logo**\
Our logo means the booking was made through the Taykus app.

**Club logo**\
When a booking shows the club's logo, it means it was made through the club's own Player Area.

<i class="fa-ban">:ban:</i> **No entry**\
This icon means the court is temporarily blocked because a player has started a booking but hasn't finished the process yet.

The player has 5 minutes to complete the booking, although this time can be changed in the settings. If they don't finish within that time, the block disappears automatically.

You can also recognise these bookings because they appear in grey in the calendar.
