# How to move a booking

You can move a booking to another court, time or day because of a tournament, an event, maintenance or because the players ask you to. There are two ways to do it: by dragging it or from its menu.

Follow these steps:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Calendar

*   Go to the left-hand menu and select **Calendar**.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (17).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Option 1: Move the booking by dragging it

* Click the booking and hold the mouse button down.
*   Drag it to the new court or time.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (18).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Option 2: Move the booking from the menu

* Click the booking.
*   In the menu that opens on the right, select :scissors: **Move**.

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (19).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
* The menu closes. Click the new date (if you're changing the day), the new time or the new court.
*   A confirmation window opens:<br>

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (20).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

    * **Update price:** recommended. Adjusts the amount if the new slot has a different price.
    * **Notify participants:** lets the players know about the change.
* Press **OK** to confirm.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

> **Important:** before confirming, check the date, court and price to make sure the change is correct.
