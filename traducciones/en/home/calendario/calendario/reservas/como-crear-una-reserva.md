# How to create a booking

Bookings are created directly from the calendar: you choose the court and time, the booking type, fill in the details and save.

Follow these steps:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Calendar

* Go to the left-hand menu and select **Calendar**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (65).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Select the court, time and booking type

* Click the court and time where you want to make the booking.
* A window opens where you choose the booking type.\
  ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (66).png>)
* Click the booking type you need.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Booking details

* The booking form opens:

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (67).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* **Player:** search for the booking holder. It's optional.
* **Court:** it's already selected, but you can change it here if you picked the wrong one.
* **Duration:** choose one of the configured durations on the right.
* **Notes and comments:** to leave any note about the booking.
* **Send communications:** ticked by default; it notifies the participants of any change.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Save the booking

Choose how you want to save it:

* **Save and Pay:** saves the booking and takes you straight to the POS to charge for it.
* **Save and New:** saves the booking and opens a new one at the next available time.
* **Save** <i class="fa-floppy-disk" style="color:blue;">:floppy-disk:</i>: saves the booking and takes you back to the calendar.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

The booking appears in the calendar on the court and at the time you chose.\
![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (100).png>)
