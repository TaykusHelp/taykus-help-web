# How to see a booking's history

A booking's history shows everything that has happened to it and who did it: who created it, who changed it, who joined, who left or was removed and who cancelled it.

> The booking's payments don't appear in the history.

You can get to the history from the calendar or from the bookings list.

## From the calendar

Below are the steps to follow:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Open the booking

*   Go to the left-hand menu and select **Calendar**.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (65).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* Click the booking.
*   In the menu on the right, click **Details**.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/partido-detalles.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### See the history

*   Go to the **History** tab.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/reserva-historico.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## From the bookings list

This way also works for cancelled bookings, which no longer appear in the calendar.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Find the booking

* Go to the left-hand menu, select **Calendar** and, in the drop-down, choose **Bookings**.
* Find the booking in the list. You can use the filters in each column.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### See the history

* Click the booking's **Id**.
* Go to the **History** tab.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

To see only cancelled bookings, see [How to see cancelled bookings](como-ver-las-reservas-canceladas.md).
