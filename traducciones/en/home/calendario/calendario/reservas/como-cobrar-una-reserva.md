# How to charge for a booking

Charging for bookings from the calendar records every payment in the system and avoids discrepancies in the cash register.

Follow these steps:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Calendar

*   Go to the left-hand menu and select **Calendar**.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (101).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Select the booking

* Click the booking you want to charge for.
* Select the player or players who are going to pay.
*   In the menu that opens, choose **Pay booking**.

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (84).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Take the payment at the POS

* The system takes you to the POS automatically.
* Select the payment method: cash, card or wallet voucher (if the player has one).

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (102).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*   When the payment is complete, the ticket changes to **Paid**.

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (86).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

> **Important:** check the amount and payment method before completing the payment so that the cash register balances at the end of the day.
