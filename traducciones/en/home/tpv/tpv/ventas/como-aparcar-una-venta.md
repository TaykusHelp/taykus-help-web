# How to park a sale

Parking a sale in the POS lets you pause a transaction in progress to serve another customer and pick it up again later without losing any information. It's a useful tool for busy moments at the till. Here's how to use it step by step.

### How do I park a sale?

If you already have the POS open with a product, either from a booking (opening the POS from the calendar) or from a direct sale, follow these steps:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### **Park the sale**

*   At the top of the POS screen, click **Park sale**.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (14).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### **Confirmation**

* The sale is parked automatically.
*   The ticket changes from green to white, meaning the POS is available again for another transaction.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (15).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>


{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

Once parked, the sale stays saved in the system until you decide to pick it up again. This feature lets you free up the POS without losing the transaction in progress and makes it easier to handle several customers at busy times.
