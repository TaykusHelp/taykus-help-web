# How to open the cash register from the POS

Opening the cash register from the POS lets you record the starting amount for the day and keep proper control of cash movements. Doing this at the start of the day helps avoid discrepancies and keeps cash tracking organised. In this article we show you how to do it step by step.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### **Go to the POS**

* Go to the left-hand menu and select **POS.**
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Select the cash register

* If the cash register is closed, a window with the available cash registers appears automatically when you open the POS.
*   Click the cash register you want to open.<br>

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (17).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Open the cash register

* After you select the cash register, the system takes you to the cash register summary page.
*   Select **Transactions.**<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (19).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* Click **Open**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Enter the opening float

* A window opens where you enter the cash you start the day with.\
  ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (18).png>)
* If you don't want to record any starting cash, leave the field blank.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Confirm the opening

* Click <mark style="color:$tint;">**OK**</mark> to open the cash register
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

Once the cash register is open, you can start recording sales and transactions as normal. Remember to always open it at the start of the day to keep proper control of the cash and avoid discrepancies at closing time.
