# How to send a ticket from the POS

The **Send ticket** option in the POS lets you quickly and easily email the sales receipt to the player. After a sale, the system sends it automatically to the address in their profile. This feature also lets you resend it manually from the POS and change the destination email if the customer wants to receive it at another address.

In this article we show you how to use this option step by step.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Find the sale

* You can find the sale from the POS itself, from the calendar or from **Sales lines**.
* Once you've found it, open it in the POS.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Send the ticket

*   Click **Send Ticket**.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   The system shows a pop-up window with the details from the player's profile.

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
* From here you can edit the email address the ticket will be sent to.
* Press **Ok** to send it.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Check

* Go to the left-hand menu and select Communications / Emails.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* In this section you can see the list of emails sent by the system.
*   Find the email and check that the **Sending status** column shows it as **Sent**.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (3).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

Resending the ticket from the POS makes sure the player receives their receipt, even if the email address needs to be changed. Checking the sending status in **Communications → Emails** confirms the message has left the system and lets you follow up properly.
