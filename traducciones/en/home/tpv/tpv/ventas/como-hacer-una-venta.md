# How to make a sale

The POS lets you record sales of products and services quickly and securely, making sure every transaction is correctly reflected in the cash register. Although the process is simple, it's important to follow the right steps to avoid payment errors or discrepancies.

In this article we explain how to make a sale from the POS step by step.



{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to the POS

* Go to the left-hand menu and select **POS**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Find the player or customer

*   Use the search box in the top right corner to find the customer you're selling to.<br>

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (20).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
*   If it's a one-off sale and no details will be recorded, select **Anonymous customer**.<br>

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (21).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Select the product

* Select the product you want to sell from the list.
*   Items marked as **Quick sale** appear directly on this screen, where shown in the image.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (33).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   You can also find them by selecting their **product type**. When you click the name of the type, all the items in that category are shown.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (34).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>


*   Another option is to use the **product search**. Click the search box and type the product name or the barcode number if it has been registered.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (35).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Check and take payment

* Check that all the items you want appear correctly on the ticket.
*   Once confirmed, select the customer's chosen payment method and complete the sale.<br>

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (23).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

Making sales correctly from the POS ensures payments are recorded accurately and avoids discrepancies in the cash register. Before completing any transaction, always check the ticket to make sure the items and the amount are correct.
