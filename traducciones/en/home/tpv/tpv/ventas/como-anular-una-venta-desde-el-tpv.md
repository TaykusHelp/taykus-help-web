# How to cancel a sale from the POS

The **Cancel sale** option in the POS lets you cancel transactions recorded by mistake and keep proper control of the cash register. It's especially useful when the wrong amount has been charged, the wrong product has been selected or a sale needs to be reversed before closing. Cancelling from the POS itself ensures the transaction is reflected in an orderly way in the system and avoids later discrepancies.

In this article we show you how to do it step by step.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Find the sale

* You can find the sale from the POS itself, from the calendar or from **Sales lines**.
* Once you've found it, open it in the POS.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Cancel the sale

*   Click **Cancel sale**.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (4).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   Before confirming, the system shows a confirmation warning.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (5).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* Press **Yes** to complete the cancellation.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Check

*   After cancelling the sale, you can check it worked by making sure the ticket shows **€0** and is crossed out.<br>

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (6).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>


*   When you leave the POS, the system automatically takes you to **Sales lines**, where the transaction is shown in **red**, meaning it has been recorded as cancelled.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (7).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

This quick check confirms that the transaction has been saved correctly in the system.
