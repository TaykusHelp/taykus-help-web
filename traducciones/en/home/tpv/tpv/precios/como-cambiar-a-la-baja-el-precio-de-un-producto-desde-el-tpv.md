# How to lower the price of a product from the POS

Lowering a product's price from the POS lets you adjust a one-off sale without changing the item's general price in the system. This option is useful for goodwill adjustments or for sorting out problems when taking payment. Here's how to do it step by step.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to the POS

* Go to the left-hand menu and select **POS**, or click the booking and select **Pay booking**. Both options take you to the POS.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Find the player or customer

*   If you're not paying for a booking and haven't already selected the player, use the search box in the top right corner to find them.<br>

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (20).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
*   If it's a one-off sale and no details will be recorded, select **Anonymous customer**.<br>

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (21).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Select the product

* If it isn't a booking, choose the product you want to sell from the list.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Change the price

* Click the product name on the ticket.
*   The line is highlighted in green and you can enter the new price.<br>

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (24).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Check and take payment

*   Check that the new price appears correctly on the ticket and take the payment with the customer's chosen payment method.<br>

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (25).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
* The new price is shown on the ticket as a discount applied to the original amount.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

Once the price has been changed, you can continue the sale as normal. The adjustment is recorded on the ticket as a discount, so the transaction is correctly reflected in the cash register.
