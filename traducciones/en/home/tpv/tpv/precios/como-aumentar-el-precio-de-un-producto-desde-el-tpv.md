# How to increase the price of a product from the POS

The POS only lets you change a product's price downwards. If you need to increase the amount of a one-off sale, you can do it by adding an open product with the difference.

The following video shows you how:

### Explainer video

{% embed url="https://youtu.be/X6QTBKdHrGs" %}

Here's how to increase the amount of a ticket step by step:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to the POS

* Go to the left-hand menu and select **POS**, or click the booking and select **Pay booking**. Both options take you to the POS.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Find the player or customer

*   If you're not paying for a booking and haven't already selected the player, use the search box in the top right corner to find them.<br>

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (20).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
*   If it's a one-off sale and no details will be recorded, select **Anonymous customer**.<br>

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (21).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Select the product

* If it isn't a booking, choose the product you want to sell from the list.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Add an open product

*   Click **Open Price.**<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (26).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* The following window pops up for you to fill in.


    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (27).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

    * **Description:** enter the name or reason for the price increase. This text is what will appear on the ticket.
    * **Price:** enter the amount you want to add to the ticket.
    * **Product type:** select a category in the drop-down if you want to classify this open product. This field is optional.
    * **Taxes:** select the tax rate to apply to this open product.
* Click **OK** to add this charge to the ticket.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Check and take payment

*   Check that the new price appears correctly on the ticket and take the payment with the customer's chosen payment method.<br>

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (28).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

Adding an open product lets you adjust the final amount without changing the item's base price in the system. This way, the adjustment is recorded correctly on the ticket and in the cash register.
