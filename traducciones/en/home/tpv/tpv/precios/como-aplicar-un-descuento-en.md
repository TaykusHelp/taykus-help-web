# How to apply a discount in %

Applying discounts from the POS lets you adjust the amount of a sale quickly and in a controlled way, whether for promotions, problems or as a goodwill gesture. Here's how to apply a discount step by step.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to the POS

* Go to the left-hand menu and select **POS**, or click the booking and select **Pay booking**. Both options take you to the POS.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Find the player or customer

*   If you're not paying for a booking and haven't already selected the player, use the search box in the top right corner to find them.<br>

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (20).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
*   If it's a one-off sale and no details will be recorded, select **Anonymous customer**.<br>

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (21).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Select the product

* If it isn't a booking, choose the product you want to sell from the list.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Apply the discount

*   On the POS keypad, enter the discount percentage you want to apply and press the **%** symbol to apply it automatically.<br>

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (22).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Check and take payment

*   Check that the discount appears correctly on the ticket and take the payment with the customer's chosen payment method.<br>

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (23).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

Once the discount has been applied, the sale continues as normal. Using this feature correctly ensures that price adjustments are recorded and avoids errors when closing the cash register.
