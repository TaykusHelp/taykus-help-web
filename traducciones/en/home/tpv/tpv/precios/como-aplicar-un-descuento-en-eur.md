# How to apply a discount in €

From the POS you can apply a direct discount in euros to a sale before taking the payment. This is useful when the club needs to adjust the final amount because of a promotion, a goodwill gesture, a one-off correction or any other situation where a specific amount needs to be deducted.

Here's how to apply a discount in euros step by step.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to the POS

* Go to the left-hand menu and select **POS**, or click the booking and select **Pay booking**. Both options take you to the POS.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Find the player or customer

*   If you're not paying for a booking and haven't already selected the player, use the search box in the top right corner to find them.<br>

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (20).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
*   If it's a one-off sale and no details will be recorded, select **Anonymous customer**.<br>

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (21).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Select the product

* If it isn't a booking, choose the product you want to sell from the list.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Apply the discount

* On the POS keypad, enter the amount in euros you want to deduct and press the **€** symbol to apply the discount automatically.
* For example, to deduct **€5**, enter `5` on the POS keypad and press **€**.\
  ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (36).png>)
* The discount is applied directly to the ticket amount.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Check and take payment

* Check that the discount appears correctly on the ticket and take the payment with the customer's chosen payment method.\
  ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (37).png>)
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

Once the discount has been applied and the payment taken, the sale is recorded with the final amount. Using this option correctly lets you adjust prices in a one-off, controlled way, while the ticket and the cash register closing are correctly reflected in the system.
