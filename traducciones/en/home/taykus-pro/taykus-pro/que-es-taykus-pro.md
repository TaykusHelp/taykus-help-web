# What is Taykus Pro

Taykus Pro is Taykus's app for the club's staff: management, reception and coaches. From their phone they can see the club's calendar, manage bookings, check their class schedule and chat with their colleagues.

> **Important:** it used to be called **Taykus Coach** and was only for coaches. If you already had it installed, it's the same app.

## What you can do

* **Home:** your upcoming classes and a summary of each club, with today's bookings and occupancy.
* **Calendar:** see the club's bookings and create, edit or cancel bookings.
* **My schedule:** see your classes, in agenda or month view.
* **Classes:** validate the class, take attendance, send communications to the students and add comments.
* **Chat:** talk to other staff members, one to one or in groups.
* **Settings:** choose the app's appearance (light or dark) and check whether notifications are turned on.
* If you work at several clubs, you can add them all to the app.

Each staff member only sees what their role allows: anything they don't have permission for doesn't appear in the app's menu. The club decides this in **Settings › Taykus Pro Permissions**. See [How to give permissions in Taykus Pro](https://app.gitbook.com/s/V0h7jTfpeuZY1RmPEtNK/configuracion/permisos-taykus-pro/como-dar-permisos-en-taykus-pro).

## How to download it

* **iPhone and iPad:** [Taykus Pro on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/es/app/taykus-pro/id6804599678)
* **Android:** [Taykus Pro on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.taykus.coach)

## What the club has to do

To log in to the app, each staff member uses the same user and password they use for the Taykus panel. The club has to:

1. Create their user with the right role. See [How to create a user](https://app.gitbook.com/s/V0h7jTfpeuZY1RmPEtNK/configuracion/usuarios/como-crear-un-usuario).
2. Give that role permissions in **Taykus Pro Permissions**. See [How to give permissions in Taykus Pro](https://app.gitbook.com/s/V0h7jTfpeuZY1RmPEtNK/configuracion/permisos-taykus-pro/como-dar-permisos-en-taykus-pro).
3. If they're a coach, also create their coach resource and link it to their user, so they can see their classes, take attendance and validate them. See [How to add a new coach](https://app.gitbook.com/s/V0h7jTfpeuZY1RmPEtNK/configuracion/reservas/grupo-de-recursos/como-anadir-a-un-profesor-nuevo) and [How to specify which users are coaches](https://app.gitbook.com/s/V0h7jTfpeuZY1RmPEtNK/configuracion/academia/como-especificar-que-usuarios-son-entrenadores).

> **Important:** if a staff member works at several clubs, they have to add each club to the app with that club's login details.
