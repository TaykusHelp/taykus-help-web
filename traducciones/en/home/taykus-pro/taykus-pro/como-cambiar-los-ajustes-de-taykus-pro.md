# How to change the settings in Taykus Pro

In **Settings** you can choose whether to see the app in light or dark mode, and check whether you have turned on the notifications the club uses to tell you about changes to your classes.

Follow these steps:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Settings

* Click ☰ at the top left and select **Settings**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Choose the appearance

*   In **Appearance**, choose:

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/taykus-pro-ajustes.png" alt="" width="300"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

    * **System:** the app follows your phone's light or dark mode.
    * **Light:** always in light mode.
    * **Dark:** always in dark mode.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Turn on notifications

* In **Notifications** you'll see whether they're on or off.
* To change it, click **Change in system settings**. Your phone's settings will open, where you can turn them on for Taykus Pro.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

> **Important:** if notifications are turned off, you won't get the club's alerts when something changes in your classes.
