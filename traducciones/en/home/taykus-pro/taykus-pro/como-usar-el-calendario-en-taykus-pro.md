# How to use the calendar in Taykus Pro

In **Calendar** you see the club's bookings day by day, court by court. If your role allows it, you can also create, move, extend or cancel bookings.

Follow these steps:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to the calendar

* Click ☰ at the top left and select **Calendar**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Choose the day and the resources

* At the top, use the **‹** and **›** arrows to change day, or click the date to choose it.
* Below, each button is one of the club's resource groups (for example, padel courts, tennis courts or coaches). Click the one you want to see.
* Swipe sideways to see the rest of the courts.
*   If you work at several clubs, click the club's name at the top to switch club.

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/taykus-pro-calendario.png" alt="" width="300"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Create a booking

* Click a free slot in the calendar.
* At the top you'll see the court, day and time you chose.
*   Search for the booking type or filter by category, and click the one you want.

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/taykus-pro-crear-reserva.png" alt="" width="300"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Fill in the booking

*   The **New booking** window opens:

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/taykus-pro-nueva-reserva.png" alt="" width="300"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

    * **Time:** choose the duration (for example, **60 min**, **90 min** or **120 min**). On the right you'll see the start and end time. To change court, click **›**.
    * **Booking holder:** click **Choose holder** and choose the player. The holder counts as one of the players.
    * **Players:** click **Add player** to add the others. In brackets you can see how many there are and how many fit.
    * **Notify participants:** leave it on if you want to let the players know.
* Click **Book**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Move or extend a booking

*   To move it, press and hold the booking and drag it to another time or court.

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/taykus-pro-mover-reserva.png" alt="" width="300"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
*   To extend or shorten it, click the bottom edge of the booking and drag it to the new end time. You'll see the time in blue while you drag.

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/taykus-pro-alargar-reserva.png" alt="" width="300"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
* When you release the booking, a window opens to confirm the change.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### See a booking

* Click a booking to open it. You'll see the time, the booking type, the holder, the resources, the participants with what each one has paid, and the total paid.
* To add a player, click **Add player** on a free spot. To remove one, click the **X** next to them.
*   In **Internal notes** you can write notes about the booking.

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/taykus-pro-detalle-reserva.png" alt="" width="300"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Booking options

*   Inside the booking, click **⋯** at the top right:

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/taykus-pro-opciones-reserva.png" alt="" width="300"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

    * **Update price:** recalculates the price if something has changed in the booking type, so the change is applied.
    * **Validate:** validates the class. It appears in all bookings, but it's only for classes.
    * **Share:** creates a message with the booking details and its link, to send it, for example, by WhatsApp.
    * **Copy link:** copies only the booking's link.
    * **Copy:** copies the booking to repeat it on another day, court or time. Then click the slot where you want the copy.
    * **Move:** another way to move it. Then click the new time or court.
    * **Results:** adds the booking's results.
    * **Chat:** opens the booking's chat to talk to the players. See the next step.
    * **Contact:** sends an email to the participants.
    * **Cancel booking:** cancels the booking.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Talk to the players in the booking

* Inside the booking, click **⋯** and then **Chat**.
*   A message tells you that you'll join the booking's chat as the club and that the players will see your messages. Click **Join**.

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/taykus-pro-entrar-chat-reserva.png" alt="" width="300"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
*   Write your message at the bottom, in **Write a message**.

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/taykus-pro-chat-reserva.png" alt="" width="300"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
