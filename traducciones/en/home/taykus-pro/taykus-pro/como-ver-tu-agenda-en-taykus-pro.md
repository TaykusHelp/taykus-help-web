# How to see your schedule in Taykus Pro

In **My schedule** you see your classes at all your clubs. You can view them as a list (Agenda) or as a full month (Month).

Follow these steps:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to My schedule

* Click ☰ at the top left and select **My schedule**.
*   You can also open it from **Home**, in **See my schedule**.

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/taykus-pro-menu.png" alt="" width="300"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Agenda view

* At the top, select **Agenda**.
* You'll see your classes sorted by day, with the class name, the time and the court.
*   The green tick means the class has already been validated; the grey circle means it hasn't yet.

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/taykus-pro-agenda.png" alt="" width="300"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Month view

* Select **Month**.
* Each day shows how many classes you have, with a dot in the club's colour.
*   Click a day to see its classes.

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/taykus-pro-mes.png" alt="" width="300"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Change month

* Use the **‹** and **›** arrows to go to the previous or next month, or click the month to choose it.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Choose which clubs to see

* Below **Agenda** and **Month**, each club has its own button in its colour.
* Click a club to select or deselect it. You'll see the classes of the clubs you have selected.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
