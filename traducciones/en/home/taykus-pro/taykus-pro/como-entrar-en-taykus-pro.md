# How to log in to Taykus Pro

To log in to Taykus Pro you need the user and password you use for the Taykus panel. If you work at several clubs, add each one with its own details.

Follow these steps:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Download the app

* Download **Taykus Pro** from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/es/app/taykus-pro/id6804599678) (iPhone and iPad) or from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.taykus.coach) (Android).
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Enter your login details

* **Club:** your club's name.
* **User:** your email.
* **Password:** the same one you use for the panel.
*   Click **Log in**.

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/taykus-pro-entrar.png" alt="" width="300"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Add another club

* On **Home**, click **Add another club**. You can also do it from the menu: click ☰ at the top left, go to **Account** and click **Add a new club**.
* Enter that club's login details and click **Log in**.
*   To remove a club from the app, go to **Account** and click the bin icon next to it.

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/taykus-pro-cuenta.png" alt="" width="300"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

> **Important:** if you can't log in, ask the club to check that your user has been created and that your role has permissions in **Taykus Pro Permissions**.
