# How to use the chat in Taykus Pro

With the Taykus Pro chat you can talk to other staff members who have a Taykus user, one to one or in groups. To use it, your role needs the **See the chat** permission.

To talk to the players in a booking, use the booking's **Chat** option. See [How to use the calendar in Taykus Pro](como-usar-el-calendario-en-taykus-pro.md).

Follow these steps:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to the chat

* Click ☰ at the top left and select **Chat**.
*   You'll see your club conversations. Click a name to open the conversation with that person.

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/taykus-pro-chat.png" alt="" width="300"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Create a group

* Click :heavy_plus_sign: at the top right.
* **Chat name:** enter the group's name. For example, "Coaches group".
* If you like, click the camera icon to add a photo.
* In **Invite**, tick the people you want to add.
*   Click **Create**. You become the group's **Owner**.

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/taykus-pro-nuevo-chat.png" alt="" width="300"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
