# How to create or change a tax

Taxes (VAT) are applied to each product to calculate its net price. Having them set up correctly is necessary for prices, invoices and reports to be right.

Follow these steps:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Taxes

* Click the <i class="fa-gear" style="color:blue;">:gear:</i> icon in the top right corner and go to **Settings**.
* Click **Sales**.
*   Open the **Taxes** tab.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (49).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Create a tax

* Click :heavy\_plus\_sign:.
*   A window opens:<br>

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (50).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

    * **Name:** the name of the tax. We recommend using the percentage itself, for example _21%_ or _VAT 21%_.
    * **Percentage:** the tax percentage.
* Click **Save**.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## Change a tax

* Click the **ID** of the tax you want to change.
*   Click the edit icon <i class="fa-pen" style="color:green;">:pen:</i>.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (48).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* Make the changes.
* Click **Save**.
