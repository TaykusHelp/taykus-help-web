# How to create or change a brand

Brands are tags you add to products to group and filter them later, for example in sales reports. Usually you use the manufacturer's name (Nike, Head…), but you can call them whatever you like.

Follow these steps:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Brands

* Click the <i class="fa-gear" style="color:blue;">:gear:</i> icon in the top right corner and go to **Settings**.
* Click **Sales**.
*   Open the **Brands** tab.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (95).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Create a brand

* Click :heavy\_plus\_sign:.
*   A window opens. Enter the name of the brand.

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (94).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
* Click **Save**.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## Change a brand

* Click the **ID** of the brand you want to change.
* Click the edit icon <i class="fa-pen" style="color:green;">:pen:</i>.
* Make the changes.
* Click **Save**.
