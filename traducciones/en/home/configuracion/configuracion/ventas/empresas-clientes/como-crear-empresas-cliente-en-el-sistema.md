# How to create customer companies in the system

If some of your customers are companies, you can save their details in the system. That way you can use them on invoices, bookings and other tasks without typing them in every time, and avoid mistakes on documents.

Follow these steps:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Customer companies <a href="#acceso-a-configuracion-de-empresas" id="acceso-a-configuracion-de-empresas"></a>

* Click the <i class="fa-gear" style="color:blue;">:gear:</i> icon in the top right corner and go to **Settings**.
* Click **Sales**.
*   Open the **Customer companies** tab.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (113).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Create the customer company

* Click :heavy\_plus\_sign:.
*   This window opens. Fill it in with the details the customer gives you.

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (115).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* Click **Save**.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

The customer company is registered and you can choose it on future invoices and tasks.
