# How to change the price of an Item product

You can change the price of any item (tubes of balls, grips or other products) from the product list or from its form. When you change the **Gross price**, the system recalculates the **Net price** based on the product's tax.

### Explainer video

{% embed url="https://youtu.be/dnUlgHbHzDk" %}

Follow these steps:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Products

* Click the <i class="fa-gear" style="color:blue;">:gear:</i> icon in the top right corner and go to **Settings**.
* Click **Sales**. The **Products** tab opens directly.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Find the item

* In the **Category** column, select **Item**.
* In the **Name** column, type all or part of the product name. For example: `tube`.
* The list shows only the matching products.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Option 1: Change the price from the list

* In the **Gross price** column, click the current price.
* Enter the new amount.
* Click outside the field to apply the change.
* Check in the list that the price has been updated.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Option 2: Change the price from the form

* Click the product's **ID**.
* Open the **General** tab.
* Press the edit icon.
* Change the **Gross price**.
* Click **Save**.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

> **Important:** before saving, check that you've chosen the right product (especially if there are several with similar names) and check its **Tax type**, because the net price is calculated from it.
