# How to create or change the ticket footer

The ticket footer is text that appears at the bottom of every purchase ticket. You can use it to add information such as conditions, promotions, a thank-you message or the club's contact details.

Follow these steps:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Ticket

* Click the <i class="fa-gear" style="color:blue;">:gear:</i> icon in the top right corner and go to **Settings**.
* Click **Sales**.
*   Open the **Ticket** tab.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (46).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Write the ticket footer

* Click <i class="fa-pencil" style="color:green;">:pencil:</i>.
*   Type the text you want to appear at the bottom of the ticket.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (47).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* Click **Save**.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

> **Important:** you can only have one ticket footer. To change it, edit the existing one.
