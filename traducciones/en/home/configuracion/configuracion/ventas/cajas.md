# Cash registers

The **Cash registers** section shows the club's cash registers. From here you can create a **separate cash register for each user**, so that each person manages and sees only their own cash transactions.

> **Important:** in this section <mark style="background-color:$danger;">**don't change anything**</mark> in the existing cash registers, especially the **Online Cash Register**. Any change can affect how the system works and payment reconciliation. If in doubt, ask the support team.

Here are the steps to create a separate cash register:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Cash registers

* Click the <i class="fa-gear" style="color:blue;">:gear:</i> icon in the top right corner and go to **Settings**.
* Click **Sales**.
*   Open the **Cash registers** tab.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (27).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   You'll see two cash registers: **Main** and **Online**. <mark style="background-color:$danger;">**Don't change them under any circumstances.**</mark>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (25).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Create the cash register

* Click :heavy\_plus\_sign:.
*   A window opens:<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (26).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

    * **Name:** the name of the cash register. If it's for a member of staff, we recommend using that person's name.
    * **Type:** Local.
    * **Closing time:** the time the cash register closes automatically. If you don't fill it in, you'll always have to close it manually.
    * **Closing amounts:** the amount suggested to leave in the cash register when closing it.
* Click **Save**.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
