# How to export a list

You can download any list to work with it outside Taykus or keep a copy. What you see at that moment is exported: the records that match your filters and the columns you have visible.

Below are the steps to follow:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Filter the list

* Apply the filters and leave visible the columns you want to export. See [How to hide or show columns](como-ocultar-o-mostrar-columnas.md).
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Export

* Click the download arrow icon above the list.
*   Under **Download**, choose the format: **Excel** or **CSV**. The file downloads to your computer.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/listados-exportar.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
