# How to save a list as a favourite

If you often check a list with the same filters and columns, save it as a favourite. In the panel it's called **Custom List** and it saves everything: the filters, the columns shown and their order.

> **Important:** custom lists can be opened by any user at the club, not just the person who created them.

Below are the steps to follow:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Prepare the list

* Apply the filters and set the columns the way you want them. See [How to filter between two dates](como-filtrar-entre-dos-fechas.md), [How to move columns](como-mover-columnas.md) and [How to hide or show columns](como-ocultar-o-mostrar-columnas.md).
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Save the list

*   Click the star icon above the list, then **Save**.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/listados-favorito-menu.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   Enter a name in **Custom list name** and click **OK**.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/listados-favorito-guardar.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Open a saved list

*   Click the star icon. Saved lists appear under **Custom List**.
*   Click the list's name to open it.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/listados-favorito-abrir.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

To delete a saved list, click the **X** next to its name.

> **Important:** any user at the club can delete a saved list, not just the person who created it.
