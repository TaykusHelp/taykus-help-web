# How to hide or show columns

You can hide the columns you don't need to make the list cleaner, and show them again whenever you want. You can also show columns that don't appear by default.

> Columns aren't deleted: they're only hidden, and you can always get them back.

## Hide a column by dragging it

*   Click the column header and, without letting go, drag it upwards, out of the list. When the eye icon appears next to its name, let go: the column disappears.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/listados-ocultar-arrastrando.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Hide or show columns from the columns button

Below are the steps to follow:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Open the list of columns

*   In the buttons above the list, next to :heavy\_plus\_sign:, click the lines icon.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/listados-botones.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* **Select Columns** opens, with all the columns the list can have. If you can't see the one you're looking for, scroll down.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Choose the columns

*   Columns with a blue circle are the ones shown. Click the circle to show or hide each one.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/listados-seleccionar-columnas.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* From here you can get back a column you hid by mistake, or add one that doesn't appear by default.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

To keep the columns you've chosen, save the list as a favourite. See [How to save a list as a favourite](como-guardar-un-listado-como-favorito.md).
