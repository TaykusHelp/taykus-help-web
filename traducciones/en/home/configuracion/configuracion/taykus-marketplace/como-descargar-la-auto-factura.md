# How do I download the self-billing invoice?

The **self-billing invoice** is the receipt for the payments Taykus transfers to the club. It's generated automatically every time you request a transfer, and you can download it for your accounts whenever you need it.

### Explainer video

{% embed url="https://youtu.be/YpHGsOSMbhI" %}

Follow these steps:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Self-billing invoices

* Click the <i class="fa-gear" style="color:blue;">:gear:</i> icon in the top right corner and go to **Settings**.
*   Click **Taykus MarketPlace**.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (82).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   Open the **Self-billing invoices** tab.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (85).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Download the self-billing invoice

* You'll see the list of all self-billing invoices.
* Select the one you want to download.
*   Click the download button.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (86).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* The self-billing invoice is saved as a PDF in your downloads folder.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

> **Important:** self-billing invoices are downloaded one at a time.
