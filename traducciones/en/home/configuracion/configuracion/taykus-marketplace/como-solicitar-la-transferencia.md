# How do I request the transfer?

The online payments the club receives through Taykus aren't transferred automatically: you request the transfer when you need it. This gives you more control to review and reconcile payments.

Follow these steps:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Payments

* Click the <i class="fa-gear" style="color:blue;">:gear:</i> icon in the top right corner and go to **Settings**.
*   Click **Taykus MarketPlace**.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (75).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   Open the **Payments** tab.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (78).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Request the transfer

* Go to the **Confirmed** block.
* Click **Sale** to select all the payment lines.
*   Press <mark style="color:orange;">**Request transfer**</mark>.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (79).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* This confirmation message appears:

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (84).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> The transfer usually reaches your account in about **two working days**.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## The Payments tabs

There are four tabs in **Payments**:

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (81).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

* **Confirmed:** payments for bookings already played, which you can request.
* **Unconfirmed:** payments for bookings that can still be cancelled or haven't been played yet.
* **Already sent:** payments that have already been transferred.
* **Incomplete:** transfers that couldn't be made because details are missing.
