# How to assign a coach to a schedule

For a coach to be bookable, they need to be in a schedule, just like courts. Before assigning them, the coach must be created as a resource. If you haven't done that yet, see this article first:

<a href="../grupo-de-recursos/como-anadir-a-un-profesor-nuevo.md" class="button primary">How to add a new coach</a>

Follow these steps:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Schedules

* Click the <i class="fa-gear" style="color:blue;">:gear:</i> icon in the top right corner and go to **Settings**.
*   Click **Bookings**.

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (40).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
*   Click the **Schedules** block.

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (41).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Open the schedule

*   Click the schedule's **ID**.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (18).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   Open the **Opening hours** tab.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (19).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Add the coach

* Click :heavy\_plus\_sign:.
*   This window opens:<br>

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (45).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

    * **Resource:** search for and add the coach.
    * **Start:** the time the coach starts work.
    * **End:** the time they finish.
    * **Ends the next day:** tick it if the schedule ends at 00:00 or later.
    * **Intervals in minutes:** how many minutes each calendar slot is divided into. For example, with 30 minutes you'll see slots at 09:00, 09:30, 10:00, 10:30…
    * **Minimum duration:** the shortest a booking with the coach can be. It can't be less than the interval and must be a multiple of it.
    * **Maximum duration:** the longest a booking with the coach can be. It must be a multiple of the interval.
* Click **Save**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Check that it works

There are two ways to check that the coach has been assigned correctly:

**Option 1: by making a booking**

*   In the calendar, create a booking where you have to choose a coach.

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (20).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   Check that the coach appears in the list and that you can select them.

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (21).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* Try to complete the booking. If the system lets you, the coach is set up correctly.\
  ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (22).png>)

**Option 2: in the coaches view**

*   In the calendar, click the **Coaches** tab.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (23).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   Check that the coach appears in the grid.

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (24).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
