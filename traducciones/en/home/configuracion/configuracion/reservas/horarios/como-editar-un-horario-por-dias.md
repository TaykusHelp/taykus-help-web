# How to edit a schedule by days

You can change the days of the week a schedule applies to without touching the rest of its settings. It's useful for one-off changes, events or the club's needs.

### Explainer video

{% embed url="https://youtu.be/zTc6DgS2DAo" %}

Follow these steps:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Schedules

* Click the <i class="fa-gear" style="color:blue;">:gear:</i> icon in the top right corner and go to **Settings**.
*   Click **Bookings**.

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (40).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
*   Click the **Schedules** block.<br>

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (41).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Edit the schedule's days

*   Click the **ID** of the schedule you want to change.

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (121).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* Click the edit button <i class="fa-pencil" style="color:blue;">:pencil:</i>.
* Change the schedule's name so it shows the days it applies to. This avoids confusion.
*   Tick or untick the days of the week.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (122).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* Click **Save**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Check the changes

* Go to the calendar and check that the schedule applies on the right days.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

> You can create as many schedules as you need, even a different one for each day of the week.
