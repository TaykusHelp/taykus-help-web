# How to get an email when someone books or cancels

If you want the club to receive an email every time a court is booked or a booking is cancelled, you need to enter that email in the court's **Booking Notification Email** field. The notice arrives with any booking: the ones the club makes and the ones customers make from the website or the app.

> **Important:** the email is set court by court, and each court only accepts one email. If you want to receive notices for all courts, you'll need to add it to each one.

Below are the steps to follow:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Resource groups

* Click the <i class="fa-gear" style="color:blue;">:gear:</i> icon in the top right corner and go to **Settings**.
*   Click **Bookings**.

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/aviso-reservas-configuracion.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   The **Resource Groups** block opens straight away. Click the sport's **Id** (for example, Padel).

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/aviso-reservas-grupos-de-recursos.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Open the list of courts

*   Go to the **Resources** tab. You'll see all the courts for that sport.

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/aviso-reservas-recursos.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Add the email

You can do it in two ways:

**From the list (the quickest)**

* Scroll right to the **Booking Notification Email** column.
*   Click the court's cell, type the email and press **Enter** to save it.

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/aviso-reservas-columna-email.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**From each court's form**

* Click the court's **Id**.
* Scroll down to the **Booking Notification Email** field and type the email.
*   Click **Save**.

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/aviso-reservas-ficha-pista.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

From then on, you'll receive an email every time a booking is made or cancelled on that court.
