# How to email a coach when they have a booking

If you want a coach to receive an email every time someone books or cancels a class with them, enter their email in the **Booking Notification Email** field of their profile. The notice arrives with any booking that includes that coach, whatever its type.

> **Important:** the email is set coach by coach, and each coach only accepts one email.

Below are the steps to follow:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Resource groups

* Click the <i class="fa-gear" style="color:blue;">:gear:</i> icon in the top right corner and go to **Settings**.
*   Click **Bookings**.

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/aviso-reservas-configuracion.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   The **Resource Groups** block opens straight away. Click the **Id** of **Coaches**.

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/aviso-reservas-grupos-de-recursos.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Open the list of coaches

*   Go to the **Resources** tab. You'll see all the club's coaches.

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/aviso-profesores-recursos.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Add the email

You can do it in two ways:

**From the list (the quickest)**

* Scroll right to the **Booking Notification Email** column.
* Click the coach's cell, type their email and press **Enter** to save it.

**From each coach's profile**

* Click the coach's **Id**.
* In the **Booking Notification Email** field, type their email.
*   Click **Save**.

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/aviso-profesores-ficha.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

From then on, the coach will receive an email every time a booking with them is made or cancelled.

> **Important:** when you book a coach, the related court is booked too. If the court has the club's email and the coach has their own, the notice reaches both. To add the club's email to the courts, see this article:
>
> <a href="como-recibir-un-email-cuando-alguien-reserva-o-cancela.md" class="button primary">How to get an email when someone books or cancels</a>

> Bookings with a coach are made by the club. They can only be made from the website if the club has turned it on, and classes can't be booked from the app.
