# How to switch between your account and your associates' in the Customer Area

If you are the responsible customer for other people (for example, your children), you can manage their accounts from yours in your club's Customer Area, without logging out, and pay for their bookings and purchases with your voucher or your card.

Below are the steps to follow:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Log in

* Log in to your club's Customer Area with your username and password.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Switch account

* Click your name, at the top of the page.
*   A drop-down opens with your account and your associates'. Select the account you want to use.

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/zona-clientes-cambiar-cuenta.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Pay with your voucher or your card

* From the associate's account, book or buy as usual. When paying, your voucher and your saved card appear under **Payment methods**.
*   If you have a voucher, it appears under **Payable with voucher**. Choose the voucher in **Select the voucher** and click **Pay**.

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/zona-clientes-pagar-con-bono.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   Your saved card appears with your name below it. Select it to pay with it.

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/zona-clientes-medios-de-pago.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

> **Important:** if the associated person doesn't have their own email, they can only log in to the Customer Area through your account.
