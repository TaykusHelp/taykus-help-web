# Active students

The **Active students** section shows, in a single list, all the students currently enrolled in academy courses. It brings their information together in one place, so you don't have to go group by group to find someone. It's the quickest way to view and manage all the players who are still active.

To open this section, just follow these steps:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Academy

* Go to the left-hand menu and click Academy.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Go to Active Students

*   In the drop-down that opens, click **Active Students.**<br>

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (5).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* Once inside, you can see all the information for all students in a single list.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
