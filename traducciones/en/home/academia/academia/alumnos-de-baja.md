# Students who have left

The **Students who have left** section shows, in a single list, all the players who are no longer active in the academy. As well as checking their history without going group by group, it helps you easily find people who could rejoin or be offered a promotion. It's a quick tool for winning students back and keeping track of them.

To open this section, just follow these steps:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Academy

* Go to the left-hand menu and click Academy.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Go to Students Who Have Left

* In the drop-down that opens, click **Students Who Have Left.**\
  ![](<../.gitbook/assets/image (20).png>)
* Once inside, you can see all the information for all students in a single list.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
