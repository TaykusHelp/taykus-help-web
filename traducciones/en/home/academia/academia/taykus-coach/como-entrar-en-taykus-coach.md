# How to log in to Taykus Coach

To log in to Taykus Coach you need the login details your club gives you: they're the same ones you use for the coaches' web address. If you teach at several clubs, add each one with its own details.

Follow these steps:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Download the app

* Download **Taykus Coach** from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/es/app/taykus-coach/id6804599678) (iPhone and iPad) or from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.taykus.coach) (Android).
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Enter your login details

* **Club:** your club's name.
* **User:** your email.
* **Password:** the one the club gave you.
*   Click **Log in**.

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/taykus-coach-entrar.png" alt="" width="300"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Add another club

* At the bottom, click **Account**.
* Under **My clubs**, click **Add a new club**.
* Enter that club's login details and click **Log in**.
*   To remove a club from the app, click the bin icon next to it.

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/taykus-coach-cuenta.png" alt="" width="300"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

> **Important:** if you can't log in, ask the club to check that your user and your coach resource have been created, and that they're linked in Settings › Academies.
