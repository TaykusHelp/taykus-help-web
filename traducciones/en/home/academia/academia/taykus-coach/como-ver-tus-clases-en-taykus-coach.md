# How to see your classes in Taykus Coach

When you open Taykus Coach, you'll see your calendar with the classes from all your clubs. You can view it as a list (Agenda) or as a full month (Month), just like your phone's calendar.

Follow these steps:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Agenda view

* At the bottom, click **Home**.
* At the top, select **Agenda**.
*   You'll see your classes in order by day, with the class name, the time and the court.

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/taykus-coach-agenda.png" alt="" width="300"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Month view

* Select **Month**.
* Each day shows how many classes you have, with a dot in the club's colour.
*   Click a day to see its classes.

    <div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/taykus-coach-mes.png" alt="" width="300"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Change month and refresh

* Use the **‹** and **›** arrows to go to the previous or next month, or click the month to choose it.
* Click the refresh icon (the circular arrow) to load the club's latest changes.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Choose which clubs to see

* Below Agenda and Month, each club has its own button in its colour.
* Click a club to select or deselect it. You'll see the classes of the clubs you have selected.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
