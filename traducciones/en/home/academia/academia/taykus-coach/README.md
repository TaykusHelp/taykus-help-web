# Taykus Coach

Taykus Coach is the app for coaches who teach at clubs that work with Taykus. From their phone, coaches can see their classes, take their students' attendance and find out about any changes the club makes.

## What coaches can do

* See their classes in a calendar, in month or agenda view. If they teach at several clubs, they see all of them together, each club in its own colour, and can filter by club.
* Open a class, see the list of students and mark who attended.
* Validate a class.
* Add internal notes about the class.
* Send an email to the students in a class.
* Get notifications when the club changes something in their classes.

## How to download it

* **iPhone and iPad:** [Taykus Coach on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/es/app/taykus-coach/id6804599678)
* **Android:** [Taykus Coach on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.taykus.coach)

## What the club needs to do

The club provides the login details for the app. For a coach to be able to log in, the club has to do the same as for giving them access to the coaches' web address:

1. Create their user. See [How to create a user](https://app.gitbook.com/s/V0h7jTfpeuZY1RmPEtNK/configuracion/usuarios/como-crear-un-usuario).
2. Create their coach resource. See [How to add a new coach](https://app.gitbook.com/s/V0h7jTfpeuZY1RmPEtNK/configuracion/reservas/grupo-de-recursos/como-anadir-a-un-profesor-nuevo).
3. Specify that this user is a coach by linking them to their resource. See [How to specify which users are coaches](https://app.gitbook.com/s/V0h7jTfpeuZY1RmPEtNK/configuracion/academia/como-especificar-que-usuarios-son-entrenadores).

Steps 1 and 2 can be done in any order, but both must be done before step 3.

> **Important:** if the coach teaches at several clubs, they need to add each club in the app with the login details each club gives them.
