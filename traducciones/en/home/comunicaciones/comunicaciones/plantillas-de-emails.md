# Email templates

Here you can see the email templates that are sent automatically to players from the club's website.

The list shows information such as:

* ID
* Subject
* Topic
* Creation date

These templates are for different automatic emails, such as confirmations, notifications, invoices, orders, welcome emails or other messages related to the player's activity on the website.
