# Email templates

**Communications › Email Templates** holds the templates for the emails that are sent automatically to customers.

The list shows these columns: **Id**, **Subject**, **Topic** and **Created** (the creation date).

These templates correspond to different automatic emails, such as confirmations, notifications, invoices, orders, welcome emails or other messages related to the player's activity on the website.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (3).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

To edit a template, click its **Id**.

Words in double square brackets, such as `[[CustomerName]]`, are automatically replaced with each customer's details when the email is sent. For example, `[[CustomerName]]` is replaced with the customer's name.
