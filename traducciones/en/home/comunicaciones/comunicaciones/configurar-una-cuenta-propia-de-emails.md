# Set up your own email account

To avoid relying on a shared account, we recommend that the club sets up its own email sending account.

You can get this account from an external provider that specialises in sending emails, such as Mailjet or a similar platform.

Once the account has been created with the chosen provider, the club needs the technical details to connect it to Taykus.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The usual details are:

* **Host**
* **Port**
*  **Username**
* **Password**
* **SSL / TLS**
* **Sender email address**
* **Sender name**

Once this information has been entered, Taykus uses that account to send the club's automatic emails.

> **Important:** the settings must be provided by the email provider the club has signed up with. If any of the fields is wrong, emails may not be sent correctly or may be rejected by the server.

In the Email block you can also review the sender information.\
These fields determine which address and name the player sees when they receive an email sent from the website.\


We recommend making these details easy for players to recognise, for example by using the club's name or a company email address.

This way, players can easily tell that the email is an official communication from the club.
