# General email settings

In the **General email settings** section you can view and set the technical details of the account used to send the club's automatic emails.

These settings establish the connection with the email server and make sure messages are sent correctly from the configured account.

The technical email settings are shown at the top of the screen.

These settings determine which account the club's automatic emails are sent from.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The available fields include:

* **Host**
* **Port**
* **Username**
* **Password**
* **Authorised**
* **SSL**
* **TLS**
* **Sender email address**
* **Sender name**

These details depend on the email provider the club uses.
