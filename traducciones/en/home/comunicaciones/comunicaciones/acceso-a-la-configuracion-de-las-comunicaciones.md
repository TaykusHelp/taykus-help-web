# Access communications settings

In the **Communications** section you can manage the settings for the messages and emails sent from Taykus.

In this article we show you how to open this section to review or change its options.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Communications

* Go to the settings menu in the top right corner.
*   Click Communications<br>

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

Once you've completed these steps, you'll be in the **Communications** section, where you can view and manage the different settings.
