# Where to see the delivery status of emails

Taykus lets you check the status of the emails sent to customers and see whether the system has processed each communication correctly.

In this article we show you where to find the sending history and how to check the information available for each email.

The **Sent** status means the email has been processed by Taykus, but it doesn't guarantee that the recipient's email provider has delivered it to their inbox.
