# Using the Taykus email account

Some clubs may have their emails set up to be sent through a Taykus account.

This option lets the system send automatic emails without the club having to set up its own account. However, this account may be shared by most clubs and handle a high volume of emails.

In these cases, email providers such as Gmail may identify the account as a bulk sender and block, reject or filter certain messages. For this reason, even if the email shows as sent successfully from Taykus, the recipient may not receive it or the message may end up in the spam folder.

This doesn't necessarily mean there was a sending error on the platform; it's a restriction applied by the recipient's email provider.

To avoid this, the club can send from its own account. See [How to set up the club's email account](como-configurar-la-cuenta-de-correo-del-club.md).
