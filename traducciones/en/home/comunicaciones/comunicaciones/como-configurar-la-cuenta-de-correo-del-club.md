# How to set up the club's email account

The club's automatic emails (confirmations, invoices, reminders…) are sent from the email account set up in Taykus. It can be the Taykus account or the club's own account, signed up with an email sending provider such as Mailjet or similar.

We recommend using your own account: the Taykus account is shared by many clubs, and some providers, such as Gmail, may send its emails to spam or block them. To find out more, see [Using the Taykus email account](uso-de-la-cuenta-de-correo-taykus.md).

> **Important:** this is usually set up by the Taykus team. If you want to change it and have any questions, contact support.

Below are the steps to follow:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Communications

* Click the ⚙ icon in the top right corner and go to **Settings**.
*   Click **Communications**. The **Emails** tab opens.

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Edit the account

*   Click the <i class="fa-pen">:pen:</i> icon.

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Account details

Enter the details your email provider has given you:

* **HOST**, **Port**, **Username** and **Password:** the details for connecting to the provider's server.
* **Ssl** and **Tls:** the type of secure connection the provider specifies.
* **Sender Email Address** and **Sender Name:** the address and name the customer will see when they receive an email from the club. Use details that are easy to recognise, for example the club's name and a company email address, so the customer knows it's an official email.

> **Important:** enter the details exactly as the provider gives them to you. If any of them is wrong, emails may not be sent or may be rejected.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Save

* Click **Save**.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

From then on, Taykus will send the club's automatic emails from that account.
