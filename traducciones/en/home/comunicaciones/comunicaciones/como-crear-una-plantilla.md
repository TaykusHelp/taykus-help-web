# How to create a template

Email templates let you save the emails you send often so you don't have to write them every time. When you send an email to your players, you choose the template and the subject and text are filled in automatically.

### Explainer video

{% embed url="https://youtu.be/CQdKu3j7PsA" %}

Below are the steps to follow:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Go to Email Templates

* Go to the left-hand menu and select **Communications**.
* In the drop-down, choose **Email Templates**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Create the template

*   Click :heavy\_plus\_sign:. The **Add Email Template** window opens.

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/plantilla-nueva-boton.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Subject and topic

*   In the **General** block, fill in these fields:

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/plantilla-nueva-asunto-tema.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

    * **Subject:** the subject of the email, which the customer will see when they receive it. For example, _Welcome to the club_.
    * **Topic:** the template's name. Enter one that identifies it: it's the one you'll see in the list when you choose the template to send an email. Required field.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Email text

* In the **Detail** block, write the text in **Message body**. You can format it with the toolbar (bold, lists, colours, images…).
*   To add a customer detail, such as their name, place the cursor where you want it to appear and choose it in the **Select replacement to insert** drop-down.

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/plantilla-nueva-reemplazos.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* A word in double square brackets is added, for example `[[Customer.Name]]`. When the email is sent, it's replaced with each customer's details.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Save

*   Click **Save**. The template will appear in the **Email Templates** list.

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/plantilla-nueva-guardar.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## Use the template when sending an email

The full steps for sending an email to several players are in [How to send a bulk email to customers](../../jugadores/jugadores/como-enviar-un-correo-masivo-a-clientes.md). In short:

* Go to the left-hand menu, select **Players** and tick the players you want to send the email to.
* Click the envelope icon and choose **Email**.
*   In **Template**, choose the template by its topic. The subject and text are filled in automatically.

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/plantilla-usar-al-enviar.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* Check the email, change anything you need and click **OK** to send it.

To add an image to the email, see [How to add an image to an email](como-adjuntar-una-imagen-a-un-mail.md).
